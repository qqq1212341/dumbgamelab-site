Tiny pixel amusement park game: pull levers for rides and make snacks by hand

동물 손님이 찾아오는 놀이공원

삐죽랜드

개구리·구름·식빵 손님을 놀이기구에 태우고 핫도그·츄러스·음료를 직접 만들어요.

Google PlayApp StorePlay in browser

You run a small amusement park, and the guests are animals.

Frog, cloud, bread, mole, and raccoon guests buy tickets and line up at the rides. When a ride is half full, pull the start lever. When the ride ends, use the exit lever to let everyone off. A day lasts 160 seconds, and every pull of the lever earns you money.

Run every ride with a lever

There are six rides: teacups, carousel, Ferris wheel, bumper cars, Viking ship, and mini coaster. If a ride is short on riders, its lever stays locked. Once half the seats are full, the green light comes on. Raise each ride's ticket price and speed separately.

Make snacks by hand

For a hot dog, set the sausage on the bun and squeeze sauce in a zigzag. For churros, squeeze out the dough, cook both sides, and slide them into the wrapper. For a drink, drop in ice and pour to the dotted line. The better you make it, the higher your score and the more you earn.

Tap coins, collect hearts for Heart Fever

Tap the coins and hearts floating above guests' heads to collect them. Collect ten hearts to start Heart Fever, which doubles your income for 30 seconds.

Build a five-star theme park

The requirements for your park rating are always on screen. Each time you gain a star, your park expands and new rides and decorations unlock. Hire a cleaning helper and a ticket helper, then complete the daily mission and fulfill guest requests to complete your guest collection. Once you reach five stars, open a new park and start again.

Daily login rewards

The 7-day login calendar gives you a reward every day you open the game. Your park is open for business while the game is running.

You'll like this if you enjoy

  • Amusement park games, theme park tycoon sims, and pixel art games
  • Short days: a whole day takes just 2 minutes 40 seconds

Critter Park is free and contains ads. Ads only play when you tap to watch one, and watching one starts Heart Fever right away or pays out today's net profit a second time on the daily summary screen.

Supported languages: Korean, English, Japanese, Thai, German, Vietnamese, Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, and Traditional Chinese.

New games and small updates, about once a month.
