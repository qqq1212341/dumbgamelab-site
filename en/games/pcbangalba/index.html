PC Cafe Tycoon

Take over a dying internet cafe and grow it nationwide

Google PlayApp StorePlay in browser

Your uncle left behind a failing back-alley internet cafe. From today, a cat is the boss.

Open with 6 seats, place the seats and ramen counter yourself, hire staff, and grow it into the #1 internet cafe in the country.

Your staff keep the shop open even at night while the app is closed. Come back and collect what they earned.

Placement is profit

  • Ramen counters and vending machines sell more the closer they are to the seats.
  • Drag to place. Block the aisle and guests can't get through.
  • Upgrade each machine separately.

10 staff, 10 kinds of guests

  • Read the applications and hire. Skill, Quick feet and Charm differ for everyone.
  • Start with students and office workers. New guests show up as the shop grows.

6 stages, from the alley to the whole country

  • Back alley, neighborhood, downtown, destination, streamer, national. Hit the revenue mark and the whole shop gets bigger.
  • Grow with research, and make a name through magazine rankings and events.
  • The story with your uncle and the neighbors continues at every stage.

Good to know

  • A day lasts 2 minutes, with up to 3x speed.
  • Plays offline.
  • Free. Watch an ad at settlement to double the day's earnings.
  • Progress is saved on your device.

Take over your uncle's PC bang in this idle tycoon and internet cafe simulator, and grow it from a back alley to the whole country. It plays offline, and your staff keep the gaming cafe running while the app is closed.

New games and small updates, about once a month.
