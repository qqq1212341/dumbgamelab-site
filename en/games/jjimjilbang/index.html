Korean Spa Tycoon

Turn a tiny bathhouse into the nation's biggest spa land

Google Play

Your aunt left you the neighborhood bathhouse. Even at noon, the tubs sit empty.

You start with two hot tubs, shoe lockers, changing lockers and one part-time cleaner, then grow through 7 stages up to a full spa land. Each stage widens the building and opens new facilities.

Guests don't use one thing and leave

Shoe locker, changing locker, tub, kiln sauna, then the snack counter or the sleep room. Guests work through the facilities in turn, and there are 13 different routes. Six kinds of guests show up as you grow: aunties, uncles, students, office workers, families and tourists.

When a spot is full they wait, and if the wait runs too long they just leave. Wherever the line grows is what you build next.

30 facilities, placed by you

Hot and cold tubs, kiln saunas, salt rooms, ice rooms, scrub stations, barbers, snack counters, diners, sleep rooms, massage chairs, comic rooms, kids zones, arcades, open-air baths, lazy rivers, water slides, the grand sauna and VIP rooms. You decide what goes where.

Facilities you can't open yet still show in the shop, so the next goal is always in sight.

Put them side by side and they earn more

A scrub station next to the tubs, a snack counter, a sikhye machine and a sleep room next to the kiln sauna, massage chairs next to the diner. There are 13 pairings like these, and each one raises income when you set them close.

7 stages of growth

Neighborhood Bathhouse, Sauna Added, Men's and Women's Sauna, Scrub & Massage, Family Lounge, Water Park, Spa Land. Every promotion continues the story of your aunt and the old regular.

More to grow with

Upgrade any facility up to 3 levels. Pick research like a street sign or number tickets to bring in more guests and bigger spending. Compete for rank in bulletins such as the neighborhood bathhouse news, and host events like Extra Egg Day or the Spa Festival. Cold snaps, monsoon and holidays change how many guests show up.

Each guest has a favorite facility. Win them over and they become regulars, with a story saved in the guest book.

Every day ends with a receipt

A day lasts 2 minutes, and you can run it at 1x to 3x speed. At the end you get lines for admission, snack, diner, service and lounge, so you can see where the money came from.

Even while the app is closed, the bathhouse earns a little.

Available in Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified/Traditional), Indonesian, Thai and Vietnamese.

New games and small updates, about once a month.
