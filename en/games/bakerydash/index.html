Chichi's Bread Run

Run, stack and carry bread. When lines get long, hire staff and expand.

Google Play

Stand at the oven and bread stacks up in your hands.

Pile it high and run to the display. Customers grab a loaf and head to the register.

Step on the cash piled at the register to collect it, then stand on the floor pads to buy new ovens and displays.

  • Drag anywhere on the screen for a joystick
  • No failing, no time limit
  • The longer the line, the more fun
  • Staff carry bread and ring up sales for you
  • Upgrade run speed, tray, flyers and bread price
  • Grow from the shop floor to a cafe, a pickup window and Shop 2
  • Staff keep earning while the app is closed

Sandwich loaf, soboro bun, red bean bun, salt bread, croissant and twisted donut. The shape of the stack shows which bread you are carrying.
