Spin the Wheel: Chichi's Picks

Add your options, spin the wheel, and Chichi announces the result with a mic. Lunch, dares, turn order: decide in seconds, no sign-up.

Google Play

Can't decide? Give it a spin. When the wheel stops, Chichi grabs the mic and announces the result.

A wheel that feels good

  • Flick it with your finger or tap the button to spin
  • Each option you pass gives a click and a vibration
  • Tap while it spins to hit the brakes

Make your own wheels

  • Up to 100 options per wheel, and save as many wheels as you like
  • Set each option's width from 1x to 5x and its chance in % shows right away
  • Spin and remove each picked option to set an order or split into teams

Ready-made wheels

What's for lunch?, Dinner tonight, Party dares, Who goes first?, Who pays today?, Yes or no, What should I do?, Pick a number

Share the result

Turn the result into one picture and send it to friends.

Fair results

The result is decided only by where the wheel stops. Nothing is decided in advance. Recent results stay on screen.

No sign-up, and it works without internet. All features are free (the app is supported by ads).

New games and small updates, about once a month.
