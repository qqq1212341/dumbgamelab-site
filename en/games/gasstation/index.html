Every car has its own stops

Gas Station Tycoon

Take over a closed gas station by the highway and grow it in seven stages. Each type of car visits different equipment.

Google PlayApp Store

You have inherited a gas station on an autumn country road. It has been closed for a long time.

Start with one rusty pump and grow it through seven stages into the biggest station on the road.

Every car makes different stops

A small car fills up, gets air, and leaves. A sedan fuels up, goes through the car wash, then gets vacuumed. A camper washes first, then fuels up, shops at the store and uses the restroom. Tractors, taxis, delivery trucks and electric cars also come, 10 car types in all, each with its own order. If the yard has no fixture for a stop, the car skips it. When you place a new fixture, a new car type starts showing up for it.

What you place in the yard decides your sales

Pumps range from the old pump to self-service, premium and diesel pumps, plus LPG and EV chargers. You also place air pumps, vacuums, automatic car washes, tire stands, service lifts, DEF stands, kiosks and convenience stores, restrooms and coffee machines. Watch where the line grows, then decide whether to add one more.

From an abandoned station to the best on the road

Abandoned station, neighborhood station, station with a car wash, station with repairs, 24-hour station, station with charging, and the top station on the road: seven stages. Hire staff to share the work, and finish goals one by one to earn rewards.

Who it is for

  • Players who like gas station games, management sims and idle games
  • Players who want to open it for a minute, tidy the yard and close it

Free to play, with ads. You choose when to watch an ad for rewards such as 2× earnings, and a full-screen ad may appear when a day ends.

Available in Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Turkish, German, Indonesian, Thai and Vietnamese.

New games and small updates, about once a month.
