Chichi's Cozy Merge Games

Merge matching breads, fill orders, and fix up a run-down neighborhood bakery.

Google Play

Merge two of the same bread and you get the next one up. Start with egg dough, then work your way through butter rolls, soboro buns, and red bean buns all the way to the mammoth bun.

Tap, merge, and fill orders

Tap a generator, like a dough bowl or a loaf pan, and ingredients pop out. Drag two of the same item together to make the next level. When you serve the bread a customer wants, you get coins. Each order shows the customer's face and the item they want, so you can read it at a glance.

Fix up an old bakery in 30 repairs

Spend coins to repair the sign, awning, display windows, oven, and more, one spot at a time. The shuttered shop slowly turns into a bright bakery. Fix the last spot and a grand-opening scene plays, and customers keep placing orders after that.

Bread, coffee, cake, and cookies: 7 lines, 56 items

Sweet buns, savory breads, baked treats, coffee, cakes, milk drinks, and cookies. Each line has 8 levels, and every item you make for the first time goes into your collection. As you level up, the board gets bigger and new generators appear. Merge two generators to get a tier 2 generator that refills faster.

Energy refills with time

Every tap on a generator costs 1 energy. Energy refills on its own over time, even while the app is closed. When you're in a hurry, you can watch an ad to refill energy, recharge a resting generator right away, or open a golden box. These rewarded ads only play when you tap them yourself. After you finish repairing a section of the shop, a full-screen ad may show up once a day.

Play in Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Indonesian, Thai, or Vietnamese.

New games and small updates, about once a month.
