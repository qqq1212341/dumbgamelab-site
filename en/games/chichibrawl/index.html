Trigger Trio Roguelike Shooter

Pick from 66 augments and fight through 8 stages on a single life. Giant bosses wait.

Google Play

Trigger Trio is a roguelike shooter where you pick augments and fight through stage after stage. Move with the left stick, aim and fire with the right button, and tap the lightning button for your super. Runs are short, so you can squeeze one in anywhere.

Augment run: clear 8 stages on a single life

Pick one of 8 heroes at the start of a run. Before each stage, pick 1 of 3 augments. There are 66 augments in all, including 10 evolved ones, and each card shows its name, its numbers and its rarity. The one you pick changes your bullets and your body, and augments that pair up evolve when you collect them. Some heroes also get augments of their own. If none fit, you can reroll up to 3 times per run. The map and backdrop change every stage, and you see the next opponent's tier and augment count before you choose. On stages 4 and 8, one giant boss waits. It sprays rings of bullets all around it and gets angrier as its health drops. You get one life, so a loss ends the run on the spot. Clear all 8 stages to finish a chapter. There are 3 chapters, and each one opens only after you clear the last, with tougher opponents.

Permanent upgrades with nuts

Spend the nuts you earn in runs on 7 upgrade slots: attack, health, reload, super, speed, rerolls and a starting augment. Upgrades carry into every later run. Each loss also adds 2% to your health, up to 20%, and as you keep running, augments that began locked open up.

Boss Rush

Collect 300 trophies to unlock Boss Rush. You fight three giant bosses back to back, and before each fight you pick 1 of 3 augments.

Side modes: 3v3 gem grab and knockout

Three players fight on each team. In gem grab, gems spawn from the mine in the middle of the map. Carry 10 gems and hold out through the countdown to win. There are 4 maps. A player who goes down drops their gems on the floor, so the other team can grab them. Hide in the bushes or shoot from behind a wall. Your call. Knockout is a round-based mode where you can't respawn once you go down.

Collect 8 heroes

Every hero has a different weapon and super. Save up coins to unlock new heroes, stack trophies with each win, and swap looks with skins.

Trophy road

Trophies unlock rewards along the road, and daily gifts and quests bring in more coins.

Practice league

Your teammates and opponents are bots. The more trophies you earn, the tougher the bots get.

Settings

Turn sound effects and music on or off separately.

New games and small updates, about once a month.
