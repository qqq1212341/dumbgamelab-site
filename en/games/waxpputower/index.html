Wakppu Tower

Cozy ASMR jump game! Climb 5 squishy theme worlds with Slipper Duck

Google Play

A cozy ASMR jump game where you climb at your own pace, listening to the sound of every squishy step. Move with your left thumb, jump with your right, and that's it. With the soft sound and feel of keycaps and squishy steps, it's an easy ASMR game for stress relief.

Climb through 5 theme worlds in order: Mint Choco, Choco, Candy, Ice Cube and Space. Each world has Butter Bar Squishy (now comes in all kinds of shapes, sinks in and slowly rises back), Keycap (the key you step on presses down and springs back, the "keyboard" in the title), and WaxPpu Ball (step on it and the wax inside cracks crisp under its clear rubber skin, crumbling more the more you press). The Choco world sinks a little and drops if you stand too long, Ice Cube is slippery, and Space has low gravity for a floaty jump.

Reach a world's summit, or watch a rewarded ad, to unlock the next one. Miss a step and there's no teleport: you fall all the way down, so climbing back up is half the fun. At the summit there's a giant squishy you can stomp freely.

Climb to the top with the adorable Slipper Duck. Available in 8 languages, no in-app purchases. This game shows ads.

New games and small updates, about once a month.
