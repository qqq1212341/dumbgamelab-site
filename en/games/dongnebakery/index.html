Chichi's Idle Bakery Tycoon

Bake bread in the oven, stock the shelves, and make new breads from dough, filling and toppings.

Google Play

You have reopened the small alley bakery shop your grandmother used to run.

Start with one oven and one shelf, then grow it through six stages into a bakery people travel across the country to visit. An idle bakery simulator set in a Korean neighborhood.

Mix dough, filling and topping into new bread

At the oven bench, pick a dough, a filling and a topping, then bake. 24 breads have names, like red bean buns, salt bread, twisted donuts and croissants, and each one you make for the first time goes into your collection. A combination with no name still bakes, and comes out as the bread baked today. More ingredients open up as the shop grows.

Named breads sell for more, and the price climbs a little with each one you find. The bread of the day, which changes every day, sells for extra. Customers have different tastes: some buy sweet buns, some salty ones, some chewy ones. In this bakery game, what you bake changes what you earn.

Where you put the ovens and shelves is your income

Baked bread fills the shelves of your bakery shop, and customers pick it up and head to the counter. A shelf holds a limited stock, so when it runs empty you wait for the oven to refill it. Fresh bread draws more customers, and a shelf near an oven stays fresh longer. Bread that can be seen from outside also pulls in passersby.

15 facilities and three staff roles

Deck oven, convection oven, hearth oven, four kinds of shelves, a counter, cafe table, coffee machine, milk fridge, packing station, signboard, plant and bread basket. You decide what goes where. Hire a baker, a cashier and a cafe helper to share the work in your bakery shop.

Six stages of growth

Alley bakery, Neighborhood favorite, Bakery with a queue, Bakery destination, Regional landmark, National bakery icon. Each stage widens the bakery shop and opens new facilities, and the story carries on.

Six kinds of customers

Grandmothers, after-school students, commuters, moms with kids, new-bread seekers and bakery travelers. If they like your bakery shop, they become regulars.

More to grow with

Research brings in more customers and bigger spending. Compete for rank with other bakeries in the Bakery Trail magazine, and enter baking contests for prize money. Around Valentine's Day, Chuseok and Christmas, more customers come or they buy more.

Your idle bakery keeps earning while you are away

While the app is closed, the bakery earns a little. Open it again to collect last night's earnings. There is no account or login, and the game plays offline.

Ads

This bakery game is free to play, with ads. If you choose to watch one, you can get 2x last night's earnings, an instant shelf refill, bread out of the oven right away, or a day-end bonus. A full-screen ad may appear when a day ends.

Available in Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Indonesian, Thai and Vietnamese.

New games and small updates, about once a month.
