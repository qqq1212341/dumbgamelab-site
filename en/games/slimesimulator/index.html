Google Play

Slime ASMR simulator for your fingertips. Press, stretch and swirl the slime, then mix your own. The stars and hearts inside move with your touch.

8 slime styles

Choose from 8 slimes, from CherryPop to PearlMint, each with its own color and texture.

Make your own slime

Pick a color and add-in in the Studio and check it in the live preview. Save the ones you like to My Slimes and bring them back anytime.

Mix ingredients, find recipes

Choose 3 ingredients (base, color and add-in) and press Mix to discover 24 hidden recipes one by one. Every recipe you find is saved in your Recipe Book, and you get a hint when two of the three ingredients are right.

Customer orders and lab rank

Make a slime that matches a customer order and deliver it to earn stars. Stars raise your lab rank and unlock new add-ins. The layout also fits tablets and other wide screens.

ASMR sound

Hear ASMR effects as you touch the slime, with lo-fi music and rain sounds in the background. A calm slime simulator and ASMR game for stress relief, made for short breaks.
