Room Claw: Crane Skill Game

A claw machine you have to read. The cable swings, every plush has its own weight, and a strength gauge shows how well you gripped. Read it, carry it, win the plush.

Google Play

Craving a claw machine when your wallet's thin? This is the one to open. 20 plays a day are free, and even the plush that slips out just as you think you've got it feels real, so it's good warm-up before the real machine.

Two claw machines

  • Rainbow Bear: 7 bear plushies (3 Normal, 3 Rare, 1 Secret)
  • Squishy Cats: 5 cat plushies (2 Normal, 2 Super Rare, 1 Secret)

Each machine holds 30 plushies, and the Secret one sits all alone at the very top.

Hands-on claw control

Pick a joystick or arrow buttons, whichever feels better, and steer the claw. Press the grab button to drop it. Once you have a plushie, you choose when to let go in front of the chute.

Easy or Challenge mode

  • Easy mode: steady grip, 10 points per plushie
  • Challenge mode: precise physics, 100 points per plushie plus a combo bonus for consecutive catches

20 tickets a day

You get 20 tickets every day, refilled at midnight. When they run out, watch a rewarded ad to the end to get 10 more. Ads only play when you tap the button, and there are no purchases.

Today's missions

Finish all 3 of today's missions to earn 1 stamp. Fill the 7-slot stamp card.

Collection and ranking

Every plushie you catch joins your 3D holographic card collection. The online ranking shows the TOP 30 by total catches. Your name is built from two preset words, so there is no login and no free typing.

This is a virtual claw machine game with no physical prizes or cash rewards. Your collection is saved on your device and may be lost if you delete the app. The ranking needs an internet connection.

New games and small updates, about once a month.
