Merge Stampede: Animal Battle

Merge matching farm animals to evolve, then open the gate for a 3D auto battle

Google PlayApp Store

Merge them. Open the barn door. Let them stampede.

Drop cute farm animals into the barn well and merge them. When two of the same animal touch, they evolve one step. When the door opens, the army you stacked pours out onto the battlefield and fights on its own.

Merge, then pour them out

Chickens, pigs, ducks and sheep each evolve through 5 stages, all the way from chick to Phoenix General. You decide which animals go out first, because their position and order in the well become your battle formation.

Real-time fights on a 3D board

The barn well sits at the top of the screen and the battlefield right below it. Your army and the enemy clash in real time on a 3D board 8 cells wide, so you watch the fight without leaving the screen you merge on.

3 chapters, new enemies

Fight through the Farm, the Autumn Field and the Dusk Forest. Each chapter has 10 rounds with a boss at the end. Crows and the Scarecrow General show up in the Autumn Field, and the Wolf King waits in the Dusk Forest. Clear a chapter to open the next one.

35 cards shape every run

Win a round and pick a card. There are 35 cards in three rarities (common, rare and epic), and rarer cards show up more often in later rounds. On rounds 3, 6 and 9 an event room opens where you choose an angel, a devil or a roulette.

Progress you keep after a loss

Coins stay with you even when you lose. Spend them in the barn on coin gain, extra drops, a starting card and a cheaper revive, or on permanent upgrades for each animal family. When your army falls, pay coins or watch an ad to keep fighting.

Reasons to come back every day

Animals and enemies you meet for the first time go into the animal dex and pay out coins. Every day brings 3 new missions and a daily chest, and the chest gets bigger the longer your login streak runs.

A guided first run

Speech bubbles walk you through your first drop, first merge, first sortie and first card pick.

Rewarded and interstitial ads may appear. There are no in-app purchases.

In-game text is currently in Korean only.

New games and small updates, about once a month.
