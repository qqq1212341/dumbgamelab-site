Chichi Camp

Animal guests drop by every morning. Fill their requests, craft furniture and make the forest camp your own.

Google PlayPlay in browser

Chichi has opened a little camp in the forest. It is a cozy camping game you can play at your own pace.

Every morning a new day begins, and animal friends drop by with small requests: a few fish, a basket of carrots, a bundle of wood.

  • Materials from the fields, the fishing spot and the woods keep piling up even while the app is closed, so it plays like an idle game
  • Craft benches, lanterns, a parasol, a hammock and a swing at the workbench and place them anywhere to decorate your camp
  • Befriend 12 animals: a bear, rabbit, fox, raccoon, duck, sheep, deer, frog, hedgehog, otter, owl and squirrel. Once the bond is strong they pitch a tent on the hill and stay
  • No energy meter, no timers pushing you, nothing to lose
  • The sky follows your real clock, from sunrise to a starry night
  • Rewarded ads only play when you tap them. One short ad may play after the nightly receipt, once a day

A little each day. Grow your camp at your own pace.

New games and small updates, about once a month.
