Brainrot Heist: Chichi

Collect weird creatures, earn cash, and sneak into other bases to steal theirs.

Google Play

Weird creatures walk by on a red carpet: a shark made of a cream bun, a frog living in a rice cooker, a penguin in a traffic cone. Tap one to buy it. It hops onto a spot in your base and starts earning cash every second.

Buy and collect

Rarity runs from Common up to Celestial, 7 tiers in all, and the rarer a creature is, the less often it walks by. The first guest is free. A lucky box takes some time to open, and you will not know what is inside until it does.

Cash piles up while you are away

Every spot builds up its own cash, so tap to collect. It keeps growing while the game is closed, and when you come back you get everything you earned. Your base starts with 8 spots and grows to 16 when you open floor 2. There are 12 rebirth stages, and each rebirth raises your income multiplier.

Cannot afford it? Steal it

Tap Steal to see bases you can rob. You will see real players' bases, or practice bases when you are offline. Pick the creature you want, then sneak up on the guard. Hold the screen to walk and stop when the guard turns around. Get caught and you go home empty-handed. Once you pick a creature up, you walk at half speed, so hurry back.

Your base can get robbed too

Other players can steal your creatures. Tap Lock before you leave to lock your base. If someone robs you, the game shows you who did it, and a Get revenge button takes you straight to their base. A stolen creature comes back after a few minutes.

Thieves show up too

Now and then a thief sneaks into your base. Tap it to chase it off. The shop sells 16 gadgets that launch, freeze, pin or shrink a thief. The shop restocks every 5 minutes.

Plenty to grow

There are 33 creatures in all, and you can fill them into your index. The fusion machine turns spare creatures into 5 new ones. Your base floor comes in 6 styles, and there are 6 pieces of gear to dress your creatures in. The Alarm Clock Owl and dragon event guests visit at set times, and some creatures only appear during limited-time events.

Ads and purchases

There are no purchases. Rewarded ads are optional, and you pick when to watch one. You can use them to get 2x the cash you earned while away, open a box right now, retry a heist, open a new spot, restock the shop, skip a rebirth or unlock a style. A short ad can play after a heist ends.

Practice bases work without internet. You need a connection to rob real bases. Available in Korean and English.

New games and small updates, about once a month.
