Fact #14

Your Dog Can Catch Your Yawns

When you yawn, your dog might yawn right along with you

Justin Lee, Dumb Game Lab · 2026-10-25

Short answer Yawns don't just spread between people. They can spread to other animals too, like dogs.

When you see someone yawn, you yawn too without meaning to.

You yawned just reading this, didn't you?

Contagious yawna yawn you catch from seeing someone else yawn

And it's not just a human thing. It's been seen in chimpanzees, dogs, cats and birds too.

It can even jump between species. When a person yawns, a dog will sometimes yawn too.

There's still no clear answer for why yawns spread. One idea is that it makes the whole group perk up together. That's just one of several theories.
