Fact #3

OK Started as a Joke About Bad Spelling

OK began as a joke, spelled wrong on purpose

Justin Lee, Dumb Game Lab · 2026-10-24

Short answer OK is a joke misspelling of all correct as oll korrect that survived by riding an election slogan.

You've probably said OK a few times today. But these two letters started out as a joke.

Summer 1838, Boston. A strange fad was spreading through the newspapers. It was a game of shortening phrases to initials and even misspelling them on purpose.

O.W.oll wright, as in all right

G.T.gone to Texas, meaning they took off

Newspapers were cracking jokes back then too

March 23, 1839, the Boston Morning Post. It ran o.k. there. It was short for all correct, misspelled as oll korrect.

They misspelled all correct on purpose?

Most of these joke words died out fast. OK survived thanks to this man. Martin Van Buren, the 8th US president.

His hometown was Kinderhook, so his nickname was Old Kinderhook. In the 1840 election, his supporters pushed OK as short for that nickname. Vote for OK. OK gets my vote.

That election got the whole country buzzing. Van Buren lost it. The winner was OK.

The president lost and the slang stayed

This story came out in the 1960s. Linguist Allen Walker Read dug it up by searching old newspapers page by page.
