What to open first: two apps let us touch right away

If you want to press a squishy or slime the moment it installs, start with Slime Simulator Time: 3D ASMR or Slime Simulator - ASMR Games (Pixit Labs). Both filled the screen with slime on first launch, and each had one banner ad. What set them apart was a full-screen video ad that opened at around 100 seconds in Slime Simulator Time.

The two Korean apps started differently. Mallang Ball (말랑볼, a Korean app for mallangi, the soft squishy toys popular in Korea) opened on a squishy intro card with a "watch an ad, touch for 5 minutes" button below it. Malang Life (말랑라이프, another Korean squishy app) opened in a room with a cat and a speech bubble inviting us to draw a new squishy. Squishy Magic stopped at a window asking for a birth year.

We did not measure sound quality or total ad load this time. There is no ranking here, only what we saw on screen.

How we checked, and what is missing

The four apps opened on November 21 were fresh installs on our work phone (Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5). We opened each one and watched for about 100 seconds, then relaunched it with airplane mode on and watched for 65 seconds to see how far it got. We did not tap through birth-year or terms windows for the apps.

The other four apps and our game were opened the same way on November 16, so their dates differ. We also did not measure how a squishy feels or sounds after long play, or how many ads show up in a day.

Super Slime Simulator: DIY Art comes from Dramaton. Its Play listing shows 100M+ installs and about 910K reviews, which makes it the most installed app in this post. The store text describes making slime by picking materials and colors, plus a slime gallery and minigames.

When we opened it on November 16, the first screen was a "What year were you born?" window. We did not choose a year for the app, so we never reached the slime. Relaunching in airplane mode brought up the same window. Squishy Magic, from the same developer, starts with the same window too.

  • Good It has the most installs, and the store text spells out the pick-your-materials making flow in detail.
  • Weak spot A birth-year window opens at first launch, so we never saw the slime screen behind it.
  • Best for Players who are fine entering a year once and want a big app built around making slime.

Mallang Ball: Slime & Squishy ASMR is a Korean app made by Mayacrew ((주)마야크루), with 10K+ installs and 60 reviews, and it was updated on November 20. The store text describes tactile play with slime, mallangi (Korean squishy toys) and wax balls, with a recorded sound for each squishy and haptics. At 219 MB it is the largest of the nine.

On first launch a card introducing a "crème brûlée" squishy opened, with two buttons side by side below it: "watch an ad, touch for 5 minutes" and "top up jelly and keep it." A reply under a Play review says even a squishy you bought gets 5 minutes a day, watching an ad adds 5 minutes, and Mallang Ball Plus has no limit. Relaunched in airplane mode, it again opened on the squishy intro card.

  • Good Each squishy card shows its name and how to press it (hold for 3 seconds, stir the cream), and the card opened in airplane mode too.

  • Best for Players who like collecting Korean-style squishies such as the crème brûlée one and touching them a few minutes a day.

Pick by how you want to play: press right away, or collect and touch

Whichever app you install, relaunch it once in airplane mode and you will quickly see whether it works on the go.

  • To press as soon as it installs, open Slime Simulator - ASMR Games (Pixit Labs) or Slime Simulator Time. The latter opened a full-screen ad at around 100 seconds.
  • For use without internet, Slime Simulator - Super ASMR and the Pixit Labs app opened straight to slime in airplane mode.
  • For Korean-language mallangi apps, there are Mallang Ball and Malang Life. Mallang Ball gives touch time in 5-minute blocks, and Malang Life starts in a cat room.
  • If storage is tight, Malang Life was the smallest at 68 MB and Mallang Ball the largest at 219 MB.
  • Dramaton's two apps (Super Slime Simulator, Squishy Magic) make you get past a birth-year window first.
  • If mixing and making appeals to you, our game lets you find hidden recipes with three ingredients.

FAQ

Which squishy games let you touch right after opening?

Of the 9 we opened, Slime Simulator Time and Slime Simulator - ASMR Games went straight to slime on the first screen. Mallang Ball started on a squishy intro card, Malang Life in a cat room, and Squishy Magic at a birth-year window.

How many minutes a day can you touch squishies in Mallang Ball?

A reply under a Play review says even a squishy you bought gets 5 minutes a day, watching an ad adds 5 minutes, and Mallang Ball Plus has no limit. Our screen also had a "watch an ad, touch for 5 minutes" button. We did not measure when the limit actually kicks in.

Which squishy and slime apps work in airplane mode?

When relaunched in airplane mode, Slime Simulator - ASMR Games, Slime Simulator - Super ASMR, Mallang Ball, Malang Life and our game opened a screen. Slime Simulator Time showed a full-screen ad, and Asmr Slime Simulator Games stopped at a window asking us to check the internet connection.

Which one should I pick for a slime making game?

Going by store text, Super Slime Simulator and Squishy Magic include making, and Asmr Slime Simulator Games showed a materials shelf on its first screen. Our game has you mix three ingredients to find 24 hidden recipes.

Which squishy app has the best sound?

We did not measure sound quality this time. Going by store text, Mallang Ball highlights a recorded sound for each squishy, and Slime Simulator Time a different sound for each slime.
