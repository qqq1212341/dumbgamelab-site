Liked Papa's Bakeria? Cooking games worth playing on your phone

What makes a game feel like it comes down to three things: you listen to a customer's order, you build it by hand one step at a time, and a round is short. What kind of game is Papa's Bakeria?

It's a browser game Flipline Studios released on March 14, 2016. It only opens on desktop. When a customer walks in, you rotate through four stations: take the order, build the pie (crust and filling), bake it, then add toppings.

For phones there's a separate mobile version, Papa's Bakeria To Go!. It costs $1.99 on the US App Store and came out on January 16, 2020. There's an Android version too, and it has in-app purchases, so check the price for your country on the store page before you buy.

Three things that make a game "like Papa's"

Search for games like Papa's and you'll find plenty of lists that pile up any cooking game at all. Deciding first what should actually feel the same makes the choice faster.

  • You hear the order and make it yourself: food doesn't pop out once you've memorized a recipe. You match one customer's words and add each part by hand.
  • The steps are hands-on: dough, filling, toppings and baking happen in a visible order, and you drag, hold and rub to do them.
  • Rounds are short: one customer, or one whole day, takes a few minutes. Good for a quick round on the commute.

How Chichi's Baking plays

Customers stand at the top of the screen and the counter sits below. Once you've heard an order, you do seven steps by hand: place the dough, add the filling, wrap it, sprinkle the topping, put it in the oven, take it out, and bag it. Filling goes on while you hold your finger down, and toppings go on when you rub.

If you can't tell what someone wants, tap "Huh?". The customer will list the ingredients plainly.

Then the orders start talking in riddles

At first, people order by name, like "One Red Bean Bun, please." Further in, it changes to lines like "The one that's bumpy outside and empty inside." That one is a Streusel Bun. A Streusel Bun is Soft Dough with streusel baked on top, so the filling slot stays empty.

Salt Bread is Flaky Dough with butter inside and salt on top. Those are the exact values in the game's code, and it unlocks on day 7. A combination without a name still sells, just for less.

5 doughs, 9 fillings, 6 toppings, and 23 named breads

There are 5 doughs, 9 fillings and 6 toppings, and you can also pick "none" for a slot. When all three slots match a combination in the collection, you get a named bread, and there are 23 of them. Neighborhood bakery staples like the Red Bean Bun, Cream Bun, Streusel Bun and Salt Bread come first, and later you get Hotteok, Fish-Shaped Pastry and Yakgwa Honey Cookie.

Getting it wrong only costs you a refund. There's no fail screen. There's also no timer, no star rating and no energy, so you can take your time getting one customer's order right.

A day takes 2 minutes, and the story runs 30 days

A day of business takes about 2 minutes, and at the end you settle sales, tips, refunds and ingredient costs. For 30 days the customers' stories continue a little each day, and from day 31 it's free play. You spend what you earn on oven heat, tray upgrades, more ovens and 3 helpers.

You can play without an internet connection. Rewarded ads are only there if you choose to watch one. From day 3, a short full-screen ad may show after a day ends, and there's nothing to buy with real money.

Where our game falls short

It doesn't have Papa's volume, with the work split across several stations and a series that runs past ten games. The story lasts 30 days, and after that it's free play, running the same shop. We also kept the time pressure of a line of customers light. Some people will find that relaxing, and others will find it a bit dull.

Right now you can get it on Android (Google Play), and this post doesn't promise an iOS release.

How to pick the right one for you

Checking just these three things will save you time.

  • Price and ads: the first screen of the store page tells you whether it's a one-time purchase or free with ads.
  • Customers at once: a trailer or two screenshots will show whether you serve one person at your own pace or juggle several.
  • Offline: turn on airplane mode and open it once.

FAQ

Can I play Papa's Bakeria on my phone?

The original is a desktop browser game from 2016, and the mobile version is Papa's Bakeria To Go!. It's $1.99 on the US App Store (checked November 22, 2026), and there's an Android version too.

What mobile game is most like the Papa's series?

For pizza, Good Pizza, Great Pizza is close. For bread and pies, the To Go! series itself is the closest. If what you want is hearing an order and making it piece by piece by hand, Chichi's Baking is in the same vein.

Is Chichi's Baking free?

It's free on Google Play, and there's nothing to buy. Rewarded ads only play if you choose them. From day 3, though, a short full-screen ad may show after a day ends.

Does Chichi's Baking need an internet connection?

No, you can play it offline.

Does the game end if I make the wrong thing?

No. A wrong order just means a refund, and there's no timer, star rating or energy at all.
