We ran 9 Android ASMR games on a real phone

Written and checked by Justin Lee · Checked 2026-11-20 · Device Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5 (SM-F731N)

Wakppu Tower

On 20 November 2026 we ran nine ASMR games on a Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5 and relaunched each in airplane mode. Six opened to their game screen offline, while three (Keyboard Tower, Satisjoy and Antistress Relaxing Mini Games) had a full-screen ad in front, so we never saw the game. Install size ranged from 69 MB to 302 MB. Ads decided most of it.

In ASMR games the ad opened before the sound

Being a sound game does not mean it plays sound the moment it opens. Three of the nine opened a full-screen ad first, hiding the game for close to 100 seconds, and the same ad was in front when we relaunched in airplane mode.

ASMR Slicing, Soap Cutting, i Peel Good, Pixel Bead ASMR and ASMR Organize: Fill the Fridge, on the other hand, opened to a game screen even in airplane mode, and three of them had a banner ad along the bottom. We could not count the total number of ads or judge the sound.

What we measured and what we could not

We ran each of the nine on our work phone, a Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5, and watched about 100 seconds after the first launch. Then we relaunched in airplane mode and watched 65 seconds to see how far it opens. We did not tap permission or terms windows for the app in some cases.

The phone was on silent, so we could not judge sound quality, total ad load or how the game changes over time. For Pixel Bead ASMR and ASMR Organize another app crossed the first launch or the phone stayed on its home screen, so only the airplane-mode screen is used. Our own game ran from a build already on the phone, which is not the same footing as a fresh install.

As the name says, this is an ASMR tower game about climbing a keyboard. Google Play lists 1M+ installs, a 4.5 rating, ads and no in-app purchases. It took 302 MB on the phone, the largest in this list.

Choosing by ads, size and offline

Whichever you pick, running it once in airplane mode right after installing shows where ads and loading get in the way.

  • If the game screen must open in airplane mode, look at ASMR Slicing, Soap Cutting, i Peel Good, Pixel Bead ASMR and ASMR Organize.
  • If storage is tight, ASMR Organize: Fill the Fridge is the lightest at 69 MB.
  • If you want ads with no in-app purchases listed, that is Keyboard Tower, Pixel Bead ASMR, Satisjoy, ASMR Organize and our Wakppu Tower, though where the first ad appears differed by app.
  • On ratings, i Peel Good is lowest at 3.9 and the rest sit between 4.4 and 4.6.
  • If you like climbing a tower by jumping, our Wakppu Tower opened straight to the tower screen.

FAQ

Which ASMR games work in airplane mode?

Of the nine we ran, ASMR Slicing, Soap Cutting, i Peel Good, Pixel Bead ASMR, ASMR Organize: Fill the Fridge and our Wakppu Tower opened to a game screen in airplane mode. Keyboard Tower, Satisjoy and Antistress Relaxing Mini Games had a full-screen ad in front, so we never saw the game.

What is the lightest ASMR game here?

By size on the phone, ASMR Organize: Fill the Fridge was smallest at 69 MB, then Wakppu Tower at 108 MB and Satisjoy at 114 MB. The largest was Keyboard Tower at 302 MB.

Is there an ASMR game with no ads?

All nine carry an ads label on Google Play. Keyboard Tower, Pixel Bead ASMR, Satisjoy, ASMR Organize and Wakppu Tower list ads with no in-app purchases. We did not measure how many ads appear in the first ten minutes.

How is the sound quality?

The phone was on silent, so we could not judge sound quality this time. The comparison covers install size, first screen, airplane-mode launch and whether an ad is in front.

How we checked

We ran all nine apps on our work phone, a Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5, watched about 100 seconds after the first launch, then relaunched in airplane mode and watched 65 seconds to see how far each opens. In some cases we did not tap permission or terms windows.

We could not judge sound quality, total ad load or long-term changes. Pixel Bead ASMR and ASMR Organize use only the airplane-mode screen, and our Wakppu Tower ran from a build already on the phone.
