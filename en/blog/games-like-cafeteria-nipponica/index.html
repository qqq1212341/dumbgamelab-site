Games Like Kairosoft: 8 Games After Cafeteria Nipponica, Relaunched in Airplane Mode

Written and checked by Justin Lee · Checked 2026-10-30 · Device Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5 (SM-F731N)

On October 30, 2026 we relaunched eight games in airplane mode on a Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5: three Kairosoft titles, three idle games and two of ours. Four opened a game screen or dialogue: the free Cafeteria Nipponica trial, Office Cat and both of our games. Idle Theme Park Tycoon stopped on a network error, and three others stopped at consent or nickname windows, so we could not judge them. What we opened of Cafeteria Nipponica is the free trial, which differs from the paid full version.

Kairosoft fans, know this first: the free trial is not the full game

What we opened on the work phone was the free trial (Lite) of Cafeteria Nipponica. The full version, sold in Korea as 맛집 스토리, is paid: 2,640 won on Google Play and 12,000 won on Steam. We did not open it, so anything we say about it comes from its store pages.

The trial opening in airplane mode does not prove the full version works offline. We did read that its Google Play page carries an Offline label.

Picking inside Kairosoft or outside it

Here, inside Kairosoft means three pixel-art management games from the same company: Cafeteria Nipponica SP, Dream House Days and Kingdom Adventurers. Outside means three idle games (Idle Restaurant Tycoon, Idle Theme Park Tycoon, Office Cat) plus two of ours.

Two things separate them. One is whether the fun is hands-on planning or numbers that grow by themselves. The other is the wall at the first screen. Two of the Kairosoft games stopped at a nickname and terms window, and among the outside games the restaurant one stopped at an age and terms window. If you need the Kairosoft look, pick inside; if opening instantly with no connection matters more, the evidence so far favors outside.

Names that differ by store: 맛집 스토리 and friends

The same game is listed under different names by country, so searching in English alone can miss it. On the Korean store, Cafeteria Nipponica is 맛집 스토리, Dream House Days is 드림하우스 스토리 and Kingdom Adventurers is 왕국건설 스토리. On the Japanese store Cafeteria Nipponica is 大盛グルメ食堂.

If a Korean search only returns English titles, try those names.

Where to start, by player

This order cuts down on installing and deleting.

  • New to Kairosoft: open the Cafeteria Nipponica trial first, then move to the full version if it suits you.
  • Same company, new subject: Dream House Days or Kingdom Adventurers. Both need you to clear a nickname and terms window, and we did not see past it.
  • If opening with no connection comes first, start with Office Cat.
  • If you always have a connection and want a different subject, there are Idle Restaurant Tycoon and Idle Theme Park Tycoon. The theme park failed in airplane mode.
  • For a light Korean-themed idle game on the side, try our Internet Cafe Tycoon and Korean Spa Tycoon.

FAQ

Which of these has a pixel-art look?

The three Kairosoft games (Cafeteria Nipponica SP, Dream House Days, Kingdom Adventurers) are the pixel-art management games here. The rest are idle games or ours, and neither of ours has a pixel look.

Which one grows most like Kairosoft?

Games from the same company are closest. Outside it, our Korean Spa Tycoon widens its building stage by stage and Office Cat fills a room layout, so both point the same way.

Can I try these without paying?

The Cafeteria Nipponica trial is free, and the full version is 2,640 won on Google Play. Every other game here is free to download, with ads or in-app purchases attached. We could not measure how often ads appear.

Does Cafeteria Nipponica work in Korean?

The trial's tutorial dialogue we saw was in English. The Steam version lists Korean among its supported languages, but we could not confirm the in-app language of the phone full version. Read the store description before buying.

Which of these works with no internet?

Relaunched in airplane mode, the Cafeteria Nipponica trial, Office Cat and both of our games opened a game screen or dialogue. Idle Theme Park Tycoon showed a network error, and three others stopped at a consent or nickname window, so we could not judge them.

How we checked

We opened each game on the work phone (Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5), turned on airplane mode and relaunched it to see how far it got. We wrote down only what we saw; where a window needed a person to tap, such as a nickname or terms prompt, we did not handle it for the game and noted that we stopped there. Our two games were builds already installed on that phone with saved data, so a fresh install may show a different first screen.

We could not measure how often ads appear. The full procedure is on the page linked below.
