Roguelike shooter basics

What is a roguelike shooter? One run, start to finish

Justin Lee, Dumb Game Lab · 2026-11-26

Trigger Trio Roguelike Shooter

Google Play

A roguelike shooter is a top-down shooter with two extra rules. Each run, you stack abilities by picking 1 card out of 3, and when you lose your life the run starts over from the beginning. In Trigger Trio Roguelike Shooter, the genre comes down to 66 augments, 8 stages, 1 life and 7 permanent upgrade slots that stay after a run ends.

In one line: every run is a fresh start, and your picks change it

The word roguelike comes from dungeon crawlers. Attached to a shooter, it means something simpler: you start over when a run ends, and the abilities you get are random each time.

Play the same stage ten times and you end up with ten different builds. In one run your bullets bounce off walls. In another, lightning jumps to the enemy next to the one you hit. In a third, you pay health to keep firing your ultimate.

So the fun is not only in aiming. Half of a run is decided when you look at three cards and choose which ones to give up.

Augments: 1 card out of 3, and evolutions that merge two cards

An augment is an ability card you pick in the middle of a run. Trigger Trio has 66 of them, split into 56 regular augments and 10 evolution augments.

Of the 56 regular ones, 45 change the rules of how you attack. Only 8 are plain stat boosts, and the last 3 are curse cards that give you a gain and a loss together.

Glass Cannon, for example, raises the damage you deal by 45% but cuts your max health by 25%. Blood Ultimate costs 5% of your health and fires your ultimate every 3 seconds. Deciding whether the power is worth the weakness is where this genre gets its flavor.

An evolution augment unlocks once you hold both cards of a matching pair. Take Bouncing Shot and Splitting Shot together and you get Firework Shot. That bullet bounces off a wall 2 times and bursts like a firework at every bounce, so the screen fills with bullets within seconds.

One life: 8 stages on a single breath

A run is 8 stages long and you get 1 life. A huge boss shows up on the 4th stage and again on the 8th.

With only one life, every augment pick carries weight. Stack nothing but attack cards and a single hit in front of a boss ends you. Stack nothing but defense and you can't kill the boss.

For stuck runs there is a reroll. You can draw a fresh set of three cards up to 3 times per run. The game is split into 3 chapters, and you choose 1 of 8 heroes at the start of a run.

Permanent upgrades: something has to survive a death

A strict roguelike takes everything when you die. Mobile games usually keep some upgrades after a run ends, so even one run on the subway adds a little progress, and that difference matters a lot to people who play on a phone.

In Trigger Trio, that role belongs to nuts. You spend the nuts you collect in a run on 7 permanent upgrade slots, and the next run begins with them already applied.

Collect 300 trophies and Boss Rush opens. It is not a normal run. You fight the huge bosses back to back.

Four things to check before you pick one

  • Look at how many minutes a run takes. With 8 stages, a short break may not be enough.
  • Check the number of lives. If there is only one, you have to mix in some defense cards on purpose.
  • See what remains after a run ends. Without permanent upgrades such as nuts or experience, you keep hitting the same wall with the same skill.
  • Look for the number of augments and paired combinations. With around ten cards, you will see the same combination again within three runs.

There is a separate 3v3 mode

Besides the roguelike run, Trigger Trio has a 3v3 side mode. There is a mode where teams race to collect 10 gems, plus Knockout, and the gem mode has 4 maps.

The practice league is played against bots, so it is a good place to learn alone. Learning how to pick augments is faster in run mode first.

FAQ

What does roguelike shooter mean?

It is a shooter with a different set of ability choices each run and a rule that you start over when you die. When permanent upgrades survive a run, some players call it a roguelite instead.

What is an augment?

It is an ability card you get by choosing 1 of 3 in the middle of a run. Trigger Trio has 66 of them, and collecting both cards of a pair unlocks an evolution augment.

Isn't one life too hard?

You have one life within a single run. The 7 permanent upgrade slots you raise with nuts stay for the next run, and a stuck run can use the reroll up to 3 times.

Is it the same as a bullet hell shooter?

Dodging bullets feels similar, but a bullet hell often has a fixed stage layout. A roguelike shooter differs because every card you pick changes the run.
