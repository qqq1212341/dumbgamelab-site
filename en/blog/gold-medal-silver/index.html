Olympic Gold Medals Are Mostly Silver

An Olympic gold medal has only 6 g of gold in it

Justin Lee, Dumb Game Lab · 2026-10-30

Short answer An Olympic gold medal is silver coated with 6 g of gold.

Every athlete's dream: an Olympic gold medal. But it's not solid gold.

By the rules, a gold medal is at least 92.5% silver. Then it's coated with 6 g of gold.

6 ghow much gold goes into a gold medal

It's a gold medal but it's silver inside?

Silver medals are made of 92.5% silver too. So going by materials alone, a gold medal is a silver medal with a layer of gold on top.

Still, a gold medal's a gold medal
