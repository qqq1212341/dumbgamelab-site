Your first 10 minutes come down to three things

Place old pumps up to 3, then an air pump, then a coffee machine. There is a reason for this order, and the car arrival gaps and the car table below show it.

The old pump starts at 300 won and gets 1.6 times more expensive with each one you add. The air pump (450 won) only opens in the shop once you have 2 old pumps, and the coffee machine (1,200 won) opens at 3. The game basically walks you through the order itself.

On Day 1, a "Place 2 pumps" goal shows up at the top. Around 100 seconds in, your first staff applicant arrives. Staff collect piled-up coins for you, but at first they run 15 seconds on and rest 45, so your hands are never fully free. Still, a coin pile clears by itself once it hits 5 coins or after 8 seconds. Skipping a tap costs you nothing, and in the busy first few minutes your hands are better spent filling pump spots.

Every car stops at different places

Your customers here are cars, not people. There are 10 car types, and each one has a fixed route. A taxi fills up on LPG, grabs a coffee, then uses the restroom. A freight truck takes diesel, buys DEF, then uses the restroom. A camper van gets a wash first, then fuel.

Equipment you haven't placed is simply skipped. A car with nothing on its route to visit never shows up at all. So the same station draws a different mix of cars depending on what you've placed.

Each piece of equipment calls in new customers

Read the table above from the equipment side and you get a buying order. Pumps alone bring in 6 types: compact car, sedan, family SUV, truck, camper van and patrol car. All of them have fuel somewhere on their route.

Place an air pump and tractors start coming. Place a coffee machine and taxis and EVs start coming. Place a restroom and freight trucks join, so all 10 types gather. The restroom only opens at Stage 3, so in your first 10 minutes you top out at 9 types.

More pumps mean more cars at once

By default a car arrives every 11 seconds. With 2 pumps the gap drops to 8 seconds, with 3 pumps to 5, with 5 pumps to 4, and with 8 pumps to 3. The yard holds at most 8 cars at a time.

The biggest jump comes at your third pump. The gap shrinks from 8 seconds to 5, so 1.6 times as many cars arrive in the same time. That step is why the advice is to fill 3 pumps first within your first 10 minutes.

What happens when the line gets long

Each piece of equipment has a limit on its line. Pumps hold up to 3 cars, while the air pump and car wash hold 1. If the first stop on a car's route is full, the car doesn't pull in. It just drives off, and that customer counts as a missed customer in the daily summary. If a later stop is full, the car skips only that one and moves on to the next.

When you have several of the same equipment, a car heads for the one with the least load. The count adds up cars on their way, cars waiting in line and cars being served. So when you see a long line, adding one more of the same equipment is the most direct fix.

Place them side by side for a little extra

Put an air pump within 2 tiles of a pump and income goes up 1.15 times. A vacuum next to the car wash gives 1.2 times, a coffee machine next to the restroom 1.15 times, and a convenience store next to the service lift 1.15 times. These rules link up the routes people drive anyway: fuel then air, wash then vacuum.

A price sign raises pump income within its radius by up to 5%. The effect is small, so it isn't urgent in your first 10 minutes.

What opens from Stage 2

Moving up is decided by total sales alone. The first step up costs 8,000 won in sales, and the target point is around the Day 3 summary (about 5 minutes in). Each stage opens new equipment, and new equipment means new car types.

One thing worth knowing before you start

The game is free and has ads. There are rewarded ads (double income, place now and so on) and an interstitial ad at the end of each day, which starts on Day 3. Days 1 and 2 are for learning the rules. There are no in-app purchases.

There are also 7 rare car types. Fire trucks, ambulances and race cars show up rarely and pay more, and this guide counts them separately from the 10 above.

FAQ

What should I buy first in a gas station game?

Place old pumps up to 3 first, then an air pump, then a coffee machine. From the third pump a car arrives every 5 seconds, and the air pump and coffee machine bring in tractors and taxis plus EVs.

What if no cars come to my gas station tycoon?

Most likely that car type has nothing in your yard to stop at. A car with no equipment on its route never shows up, so find its route in the table and place that equipment.

What do I do when the line is too long?

Add one more of the same equipment as the one with the long line. Cars head for the least loaded one, so even one extra splits the line. Also remember that if the first stop's line is full, the car just drives off.

I can't see the air pump or coffee machine in the shop.

Both open once you've placed enough old pumps: 2 for the air pump, 3 for the coffee machine.

Does this idle gas station game have ads?

Yes. It has rewarded ads and an interstitial ad at the end of each day, and the interstitial starts on Day 3. There are no in-app purchases.
