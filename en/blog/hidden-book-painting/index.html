Fact #37

This book hides a picture in its edges

Fan the pages slightly, and a landscape appears in the gilt edge

Justin Lee, Dumb Game Lab · 2026-11-17

Short answer Some books hide paintings on their page edges. Fanning the pages reveals them, and some books show a different picture in each direction.

With the book closed, the edge looks golden. Fan the pages slightly, and a picture appears.

It's a bookbinding craft. The pages are fanned out and clamped. Then the edges get painted.

Not inside the book. Along its side?

Closed, the edge shows only gold. Fanned, the painting comes out. The gilding hides it.

One volume of Shakespeare's plays still has one. This photo shows another book with the trick.

Some books hide two pictures. Fan one way for one scene. Fan the other way for another.

Guess I'll have to read this one sideways, too.
