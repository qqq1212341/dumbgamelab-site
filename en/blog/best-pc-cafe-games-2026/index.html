Internet Cafe Simulator Games in 2026: 17 Picks, iPhone Included

Justin Lee, Dumb Game Lab · 2026-11-01

Yes, there are internet cafe simulators on iPhone. On November 1, 2026 we found 7 in the US App Store, and the one with the most ratings is Internet Cafe Empire (4.7 stars, 569 ratings). On Android, Internet Gamer Cafe Simulator has the most installs at 10M+. On PC, Gaming Cafe Simulator on Steam is the best reviewed (391 reviews, 82% positive). We could not find an official iPhone version of the original Internet Cafe Simulator 1 or 2.

How we picked

On November 1, 2026 we opened the Steam, Google Play and App Store pages ourselves and read the prices and ratings. Prices are US store list prices, and review and install counts are the numbers shown on the page that day. A game is marked as having an offline note only if its store page says so.

We only included games where you run the internet cafe yourself. Games people play inside a real internet cafe, like League of Legends or Valorant, are a different topic, so we left them out.

Android: where the install counts are biggest

Google Play shows install counts, so you can measure popularity at a glance. Internet Gamer Cafe Simulator has 10M+ installs and 71,700 reviews, and it was updated on October 28. The original Internet Cafe Simulator 1 has 10M+ installs and a 3.8 rating, and Internet Cafe Simulator 2 has 5M+ installs and a 3.0 rating. On phones the sequel scores lower, the opposite of how PC players treat it on Steam.

Warnet Life has 5M+ installs and 167,000 reviews, and it was updated on July 30. Its sequel Gaming Cafe Life has a 3.6 rating, 17,000 reviews and 1M+ installs, and its store description has an offline note. If you want an idle game, Internet Cafe Idle Tycoon Game (Hyper Joy) has 100K+ installs and 1,870 reviews.

Watch out for look-alike new releases

Internet Cafe Simulator 2025 ($5.99) shares its name with the original but is a different game from a different developer. Only 41% of its 79 reviews recommend it, so its rating is "Mixed". Check the developer's name before you buy.

Internet Cafe 98 is not out until November 23, so there is nothing to rate yet. It is described as a retro idle game, and a demo came out first.

Our game, PC Cafe Tycoon (PC방 키우기): what it does well and where it falls short

PC Cafe Tycoon (PC방 키우기) from Dumb Game Lab is an idle management game where you inherit a back-alley internet cafe and grow it into the best in the country. You can assign each seat a zone: "fast PC", "snacks" or "quiet spot". Hire part-timers and the shop keeps running while the app is closed, and it works without an internet connection. A daily settlement shows a revenue chart and income by item.

It has weak spots too

There is no walking around in first person, building PCs or fighting customers. It has 6 stages from back alley to nationwide, so it has an end, and if you expect the detailed operations of Internet Cafe Simulator 2 it will feel shallow. Progress is saved on the device only, so be careful when you switch phones. Watching an ad at settlement doubles that day's earnings, but you can progress without watching.

Which one fits you

On iPhone, start with Internet Cafe Empire. It has the most ratings and the highest score. For a first-person feel on Android, try Warnet Life, and to play the original on a phone, Internet Cafe Simulator 1. For a deep run on a PC, Gaming Cafe Simulator. If you want to leave it running on your commute and let the shop grow by itself, PC Cafe Tycoon (PC방 키우기).

  • iPhone, idle: Internet Cafe Empire
  • Android, first person: Warnet Life
  • PC, deep: Gaming Cafe Simulator
  • Phone, short sessions: PC Cafe Tycoon (PC방 키우기)

FAQ

Is there an internet cafe simulator for iPhone?

Yes. On November 1, 2026 we found 7 in the US App Store, including Internet Cafe Empire, Warnet Life and Gaming Cafe Life. We could not find an official iPhone version of the original Internet Cafe Simulator 1 or 2.

Which internet cafe games work offline?

Every Steam game here is single player. On mobile, Gaming Cafe Life says on its store page that no internet connection is needed, and PC Cafe Tycoon also runs without internet. Many other apps do not say anything about offline play on their store pages.

How much is Internet Cafe Simulator 2?

The Steam list price is $19.99 in the US store, and it supports Windows only. The Android version is free and rated 3.0. Sales are frequent, so check the Steam page for the current price before you buy.

Which internet cafe game has the best Steam reviews?

On this list, Gaming Cafe Simulator is the best with 391 reviews and 82% positive. Its list price is $13.99.
