We Tried 8 Slime Simulator Apps on a Real Phone

Written and checked by Justin Lee · Checked 2026-11-16 · Device Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5 (SM-F731N)

Slime Making Simulator: ASMR

Google Play

On Nov 16, 2026 we installed six slime simulator apps on a Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5 and relaunched each in airplane mode. Four still opened to the slime or a first screen, one stopped at a connection notice and one at a birth-year window. We could not open Goo or Magic Slime Simulator on the phone, so those two rest on their Play pages alone.

What to open first, based on what we saw

If you just want to touch slime right away, Slime Simulator - ASMR Games by Pixit Labs was the quickest. A rainbow slime was on screen at launch, and over 100 seconds the only ad was a banner along the top, and it opened the same way in airplane mode.

Two apps threw a full-screen ad at us almost immediately: Slime ASMR : Slime Simulator and Slime Simulator - Super ASMR. Asmr Slime Simulator Games showed a check-your-connection window offline, and Super Slime Simulator stopped at a birth-year window before we saw any slime.

We did not measure how often ads appear or how the sound is, so there is no ranking below, only the differences we saw on screen.

How we checked, and what we could not

Each app went onto the work phone as a fresh install. We opened it, watched for about 100 seconds (some of that without touching the slime), then turned on airplane mode and relaunched it to see how far it got.

Where an age or terms window needed a tap, we did not tap for the game and noted that we stopped there. We did not judge sound quality, total ad load, or how an app changes after long play. Goo and Magic Slime Simulator were never opened on the phone.

Which one for whom

Whichever you install, relaunching it once in airplane mode shows the differences quickly.

  • To touch slime right away, open Slime Simulator - ASMR Games by Pixit Labs. Only a banner ad appeared over 100 seconds.
  • If it must open offline, look at Slime Simulator - Super ASMR or Slime ASMR : Slime Simulator, and know both opened a full-screen ad after launch.
  • If storage is tight, Slime ASMR : Slime Simulator was the smallest at 52 MB.
  • If you want the most-installed app, that is Super Slime Simulator: DIY Art, after its birth-year window.
  • If you want making, not just touching, our game mixes three ingredients, hides 24 secret recipes and runs on customer orders.

FAQ

Which slime simulator apps open in airplane mode?

Of the six we opened on the phone, Slime Simulator - ASMR Games, Slime Simulator - Super ASMR, Slime ASMR : Slime Simulator and our game reached the slime or a first screen offline. Asmr Slime Simulator Games stopped at a connection notice.

Which slime game has the fewest ads?

We did not measure total ad load. No full-screen ad opened in 100 seconds in Pixit Labs' app or ours, while Super ASMR and Slime ASMR opened one within 25 seconds. That is a short look, not a firm comparison.

Does Asmr Slime Simulator Games have no ads?

Its store description says no ads, but the Play label says it contains ads and in-app purchases. When we opened it, a banner ad ran along the top of the first screen.

Which slime apps let you make slime, not just touch it?

Super Slime Simulator, Goo and Asmr Slime Simulator Games all describe making slime in their store text. Ours mixes three ingredients to find 24 hidden recipes.

How we checked

We installed six apps fresh on a work phone (Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5), watched each for about 100 seconds, then relaunched it in airplane mode to see how far it opened. Some of that time we did not touch the slime, and we did not fill in birth-year or terms windows for the game.

We could not measure sound quality, total ad load, or changes over long play. Goo and Magic Slime Simulator were not opened on the phone, so only their store pages are described.
