When do hit mobile games show their first ad? We timed 8 of them

Justin Lee, Dumb Game Lab · 2026-11-15

Six of the eight games we timed showed no forced ad in their first minutes (4 to 45 minutes, depending on the game). Block Blast forced a 31-second video at the first game over, about 12 minutes in, and My Perfect Hotel started forcing full-screen ads after a notice at about 7 minutes. The rest only offered optional ads, behind a button. We measured these on one Android phone with no purchases, so treat each number as one run, not an average.

What we measured and how

We installed each game on our work phone (Galaxy Z Flip5, Android 14), played the opening by hand, recorded the screen, and wrote down every ad we met. We bought nothing and never pressed an ad's install button. The notes live in our teardown documents, and the game names in the related list at the bottom link to the pages where we publish the full tables.

The table keeps only what those notes say. Where a cell says "not timed", we did not time it. That is not the same as zero. The games were measured between 26 October and 13 November 2026, on whatever version was current that day, so a later update can change any row.

Four things we took from it

1. Wait before the first forced ad. Six of eight let the player run for minutes with none, and the two that did not waited 7 and 12 minutes. In our engine the first-ad rules are defaults in the ads policy: no full-screen ad on days 1 to 3 (a day in our games lasts about 120 seconds), none in the first 180 seconds of a session, and none until three sessions and 24 hours after install. A game can override them, but the default is the quiet start.

2. Put the offer where the player already has something to gain. Subway Surfers offers a continue at the moment you die, Hospital Empire offers a profit boost, and Eatventure offers its booster only after the menu is bought out. In our tycoons the same idea shows up as a "watch an ad for double" button on the card you see when you come back and the money has piled up. The base amount is already in the wallet before the card appears, so a failed ad costs the player nothing. A second spot is an ad button on the shop card that appears only when you are short by 30 percent of the price or less, up to 5 times a day by default.

3. Cap the interruptions you control. Block Blast's ads came after every game over, and My Perfect Hotel's playable ad would not close. We do not choose how long an ad network's video runs, so our engine limits how often it can start: at least 180 seconds apart, 2 per session, 4 per day by default, with an optional longer rest after a rewarded ad.

4. Do not pitch ad removal before trust. My Perfect Hotel showed a 15,000 won welcome pack with ad removal in the first minute, Hospital Empire shows a No Ads Pack every time you return, and Subway Surfers sells ad removal for 3,000 won in its shop. We did not measure what any of these removes. In our engine the ad-free purchase is an iOS option that a game has to switch on; a buyer sees no full-screen ads and collects rewarded ads' rewards without watching a video.

What we could not measure

Short opening runs say nothing about hour 5. We did not time the first optional ad in four of the eight games, we did not buy ad removal anywhere, and we did not watch any ad to the end except the Hospital Empire reward video we opened. We also have no ad-count data from other phones or other countries.

FAQ

How long until a mobile game shows its first ad?

In our runs, from none for the whole check to about 12 minutes. Six of eight games showed no forced ad for the first 4 to 45 minutes. Block Blast forced one at its first game over, about 12 minutes in, and My Perfect Hotel forced one after a notice at about 7 minutes.

Are the first ads in mobile games usually skippable?

The optional ones are, because they sit behind a button. The forced ones varied: Block Blast's first two videos had no skip, the third had a skip arrow, and My Perfect Hotel's playable ad had no close button for 156 seconds.

Which game forced its first ad latest?

Block Blast, at its first game over about 12 minutes in. Six other games forced nothing in the stretch we played, from about 4 minutes (Lucky Defense, Subway Surfers) to about 45 minutes (Hospital Empire Tycoon).

Is this an average across all players?

No. It is one run per game on one phone, with the game version of that day. It shows what a new player can run into, not how often.
