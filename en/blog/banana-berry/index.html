Fact #4

Bananas Are Berries

Strawberries aren't berries, but bananas are

Justin Lee, Dumb Game Lab · 2026-10-24

Short answer In botany, bananas and tomatoes are berries, while strawberries and raspberries are not.

In botany, a banana is a berry. That puts it on the same team as blueberries.

Wait, a banana is a berry?

BERRYa fruit that grows from one flower, with its seeds inside the flesh

In botany, being a berry has nothing to do with the name. If it grows from one flower, is soft all the way through, and has its seeds tucked in the flesh, it's a berry.

So grapes, tomatoes, eggplants and cucumbers are berries too. The bananas we eat have almost no seeds that can sprout, but the fruit is still built like a berry.

Strawberries, on the other hand, aren't berries. Each of those tiny dots on the outside is a fruit of its own, with a real seed inside.

The red part we eat is the base that held up the flower, swollen up.

It has berry in its name and it's not a berry?

Raspberries aren't berries either. They're lots of tiny fruits clumped together.
