Fact #43

Liquid metal helps flatten window glass

One way to make flat window glass is to float it on molten tin

Justin Lee, Dumb Game Lab · 2026-11-23

Short answer A common flat-glass process floats molten glass on molten tin. The glass spreads into a flat ribbon, then its thickness is controlled before slow cooling and cutting.

How do huge windows get so flat? The photo shows factory equipment moving flat glass.

In a common flat-glass process, molten glass floats on top of molten metal.

Even the floor is liquid?

That metal is tin. The hot glass doesn't mix with it and spreads across it as a long, flat ribbon.

Pouring isn't the end of the job. The factory also controls thickness and width.

After slow cooling, inspection and cutting, the glass can be used in buildings. Not every glass product is made this way.

Before it was a window, it floated on metal.
