The short answer: what to play after Egg, Inc.

Search for games like Egg Inc and the top results are Reddit threads. The replies are mostly a list of names, so price, ads and whether the game needs internet are left for you to look up. This post reads those three things from the Google Play pages and puts them in one table.

Egg, Inc. is a free game from Auxbrain. On the US Google Play page on October 30, 2026 it shows 10M+ installs, a 4.7 rating from about 1.41 million reviews, and it contains ads and in-app purchases. You hatch chickens, sell eggs, and grow the farm through research and space expeditions.

If you want more of the same loop, pick another idle game where the numbers keep climbing. If a different theme is fine, start with one of the five below.

Offline means two different things

In an idle game's description, offline can mean money keeps piling up while the app is closed, or it can mean the game launches without internet. The second one is what you need on a plane or a subway.

So the table has two columns. One is the Offline label Google Play puts on a game page. The other is wording that actually appears in the store description. If neither says anything, the cell reads not stated. We did not switch airplane mode on and test each game, so if it matters, launch it once yourself before you rely on it.

If you liked watching the numbers climb: Idle Miner Tycoon and Idle Bank Tycoon

Idle Miner Tycoon is the biggest game in the table with 100M+ installs. You run a mine and grow it with what you earn, which sits close to how Egg, Inc. keeps expanding its farm. It has the Play Offline label and offline wording in the description.

Idle Bank Tycoon comes from the same developer with a bank theme. Its description says outright that no internet connection is needed and it has the Offline label, so for someone who needs to play without internet, it is the clearest of the five.

Games that may need internet: AdVenture Capitalist and Idle Theme Park Tycoon

AdVenture Capitalist has a line in its description saying an internet connection is required, and it has no Offline label. For a subway ride, this one is out going by its own description.

Idle Theme Park Tycoon has no Offline label, but offline progression is on its feature list. That wording can mean it keeps progressing while the app is closed, so the page alone does not tell you whether it launches without internet.

Our own Chichi's Idle Bakery Tycoon: where it fits and where it is weak

Chichi's Idle Bakery Tycoon is a game we made at Dumb Game Lab. You bake bread in the oven, stock the shelves and grow a bakery, and the English store description says there is no account or login and the game plays offline.

The weak points are plain. It is a new release with 50+ installs and no rating yet. It does not have years of content like the games above. It also lacks the Play Offline label, so the offline claim goes only as far as the store description. Like the others, it has ads and in-app purchases.

How to choose

Going in this order saves you some installing and deleting.

  • If you need to play with no internet, start with Idle Bank Tycoon or Idle Miner Tycoon, whose descriptions say so.
  • If you want the most installed game, that is Idle Miner Tycoon at 100M+.
  • Skip AdVenture Capitalist for offline use, since its description says it requires internet.
  • If a different theme is fine and you want to try a small new release, try Chichi's Idle Bakery Tycoon.
  • Whichever you pick, launch it once in airplane mode after installing.

How this list was made, and its limits

Beyond the Play Offline label, we could not confirm from the description whether Egg, Inc. launches without internet, so that cell says not stated. All of this is what the store pages say, not a verdict from playing each game.

FAQ

What are good games like Egg Inc?

From the Google Play pages we read, Idle Miner Tycoon, Idle Bank Tycoon, AdVenture Capitalist and Idle Theme Park Tycoon are the closest idle games. All are free with ads and in-app purchases. We did not find another game with the same chicken theme.

Does Egg Inc work offline?

The US Google Play page carries the Offline label. The description has no offline wording, so if you plan to use it where there is no signal, launch it once in airplane mode first.

What is a good offline alternative to Egg Inc?

Idle Bank Tycoon says in its description that no internet connection is needed, and Idle Miner Tycoon has both the Offline label and offline wording. AdVenture Capitalist says an internet connection is required.

Do idle games earn money while the app is closed?

It depends on the game. Idle Theme Park Tycoon lists offline progression as a feature. Earning while the app is closed is a different thing from launching without internet, so read the store wording as two separate claims.

Does Chichi's Idle Bakery Tycoon play offline?

The English Google Play description says there is no account or login and the game plays offline. It does not carry the Play Offline label yet. It is a new Dumb Game Lab release with 50+ installs and no rating so far.
