Games like Egg Inc: nine idle games we launched in airplane mode

Written and checked by Justin Lee · Checked 2026-10-30 · Device Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5 (SM-F731N)

On October 30, 2026 we installed nine idle games similar to Egg Inc on a Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5 and relaunched each one in airplane mode. Three of the nine reached the game or its tutorial (Earth Inc., Office Cat and our own bakery), one stopped on a network error, and five stopped at an age or terms window we did not tap through. Egg Inc itself, the benchmark, still opened to its first tutorial screen in airplane mode.

Short answer: what to play after Egg Inc

If working without a connection matters, Earth Inc. and Office Cat are the safest bets here. Both still opened to gameplay or a tutorial after a relaunch in airplane mode.

The benchmark, Egg Inc, also opened to its first tutorial screen in airplane mode after the space intro. Some Korean text on screen turned into boxes offline, though, and we did not look past the first few minutes.

The rest differ in how far we could see, not in rank. We did not measure how often ads appear.

"Offline" means two different things

Idle-game listings use "offline" for two ideas: money that keeps piling up while the app is closed, and a game that opens with no internet at all. The second is the one you need on a subway or a flight.

This piece tests only the second. After installing, we relaunched each game in airplane mode and noted how far it went; where a consent window needed a tap, we did not tap for the game.

Which to pick, by player

Going in this order saves you installs and deletions.

  • If working with no internet comes first, start with Earth Inc. or Office Cat.
  • If you want to stay with mining and do not mind an age prompt, pick Idle Miner Tycoon.
  • If you always have a connection and just want a new theme, Idle Bank Tycoon, Idle Restaurant Tycoon and Idle Theme Park Tycoon are options.
  • If you would rather try a small, light newcomer, there is our own bakery game.
  • Either way, launch it once in airplane mode after installing. What we saw stops at the first screens.

FAQ

Which Egg Inc alternatives open without internet?

Of the nine we relaunched in airplane mode, Earth Inc., Office Cat and our bakery game reached gameplay or a tutorial. Egg Inc itself also reached its first tutorial screen.

Why did some games stop in airplane mode?

Idle Theme Park Tycoon showed a network error on the loading screen. The other five stopped at an age or terms window that we did not tap through, so what comes after is unchecked.

Which one has the fewest ads?

We could not measure ad frequency. Office Cat showed no ad window in about 65 seconds and our bakery opened one in 100 seconds, which is too short a watch to rank anything.

Do idle games earn money while the app is closed?

It varies by game. Earning while closed and opening with no internet are separate things, and this piece only checked the second.

Where does Chichi's Idle Bakery Tycoon fit?

It is a small newcomer whose saved game resumes in airplane mode, so it suits players who want something light. Ratings and installs have not built up yet and it has less content than the big names.
