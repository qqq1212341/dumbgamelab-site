ASMR Tower Roblox on Your Phone: Two Ways to Play

Justin Lee, Dumb Game Lab · 2026-11-15

There are two ways to play the Roblox ASMR tower (Korean players call it Wakpu or Wakppu Tower) on a phone. One is to install the Roblox app and search for the original. The other is a phone app that gives you a similar ASMR tower climb without Roblox. This guide lays out the difference, plus the 8 maps, the controls and where the ads sit in the second option, Wakppu Tower.

First, the name

In Korean the name is written two ways, 왁푸 타워 and 왁뿌 타워. When you type the second spelling into Google, six suggestions appear, and searches for the Roblox original run through them: ball, recommended, world 4, and how to play the Roblox version (checked on November 15, 2026).

So this guide splits the two routes: playing on Roblox, and climbing in a single phone app outside Roblox.

Route one: the original in the Roblox app

The original runs inside Roblox. On a phone, you install the Roblox app, log in and search for the game by name. The controls and worlds follow Roblox-side updates, so this guide does not cover details such as world 4. Check them on the original's own screen.

The upside is that you get the original as it is. The catch is that you need the Roblox app and an account first.

Route two: Wakppu Tower, one app

Wakppu Tower is a mobile game made separately from Roblox. On Android you can get it on Google Play. You walk with your left thumb and jump with your right, and that is all the controls there are. You climb at your own pace, listening to the sound of stepping on keycaps and squishies.

There are 8 maps in all. In lobby order: Mint Choco, Soda Pop, Sticky Honey, Candy, Cloud, Choco, Ice Cube and Space.

What happens when you fall

There is no teleport. One missed step and you fall all the way down to the floor below, and climbing back up is part of the feel of the game, as the store description puts it. Clear the first map once and the Infinite Tower opens. At the summit there is a giant squishy you can stomp as much as you like.

Ads and payments, up front

This game shows ads. The next map opens when you reach the summit of the previous one or watch a rewarded ad. There are no in-app purchases, and the game supports 9 languages.

Which route fits you

See which of these sounds closest to you.

  • If you want the original's worlds and the talk around it, go in through the Roblox app.
  • If you would rather not install Roblox, or you just want the sound and feel, a single app is the quicker way up.
  • If you dislike ads, check the original first. Wakppu Tower has ads.

FAQ

How do I play the Roblox ASMR tower on my phone?

Install the Roblox app, log in and search for the game by name. World layouts change with updates, so follow what the game screen tells you.

What is the difference between the Roblox original and Wakppu Tower?

The original runs inside Roblox. Wakppu Tower is a separate mobile game made apart from Roblox. In Korean the name is written two ways, 왁푸 and 왁뿌, and both show up in search.

Can I play an ASMR tower on my phone without Roblox?

On Android, yes, with Wakppu Tower on Google Play. You walk with your left thumb and jump with your right, and there are 8 maps.

Does Wakppu Tower have ads?

Yes. The next map opens when you reach the summit of the previous one or watch a rewarded ad. There are no in-app purchases.
