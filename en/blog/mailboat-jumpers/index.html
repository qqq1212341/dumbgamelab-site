Fact #36

Deliver the mail. Now catch the boat!

After delivering letters, these carriers jump back onto a moving mailboat

Justin Lee, Dumb Game Lab · 2026-11-16

Short answer On Geneva Lake in Wisconsin, summer mail carriers jump between docks and a moving boat. If they miss the return jump, the captain comes back for them.

On Geneva Lake in Wisconsin, a mailboat visits lakeside homes in summer.

A carrier jumps from the moving boat onto a dock, delivers letters and picks up outgoing mail.

Excuse me! Your coworker is still ashore!

Then it's time to jump back onto the boat, which is still moving. The letters have arrived. The carrier is chasing the ride.

It's real mail delivery, not a movie shoot. Passengers ride along to watch, too.

Nobody gets abandoned. If a carrier misses the return jump, the captain comes back to pick them up.
