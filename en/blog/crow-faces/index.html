Crows Remember Your Face and Spread the Word

Crows can tell human faces apart, and they seem to pass the word about bad ones

Justin Lee, Dumb Game Lab · 2026-11-04

Short answer Crows recognize individual human faces and are known to spread word of dangerous people with their calls.

A crow is staring right at you. Those eyes might remember your face.

Crows can pick out individual human faces. When they see a threatening face, brain regions linked to attention and fear light up.

I did nothing to a crow yesterday, right?

Crows are thought to pass word of dangerous people to other crows through their calls.

A crow carrying off a cookie from the trash. Crows are also known for using tools.

Ravens can even recognize themselves in a mirror. There's a reason they have a reputation for brains.

So around crows, it's safer to mind your manners.
