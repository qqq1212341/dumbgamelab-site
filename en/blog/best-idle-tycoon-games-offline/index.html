How we picked

From the idle tycoon genre, we kept games that are actually listed on Google Play and the App Store and have built up installs or reviews. This is not a verdict from playing each one for a day. It copies what the Google Play detail page (US store, English), the App Store search results and the Steam pages said on October 16, 2026.

The offline columns record two separate things. One is the "Offline" tag on the Google Play detail page, and the other is the wording in the store description. If neither says anything, the cell reads "not stated." Prices are the list price in the US store.

Why "offline" means two things

In idle games, offline usually means "you keep earning while you are away." Whether the game launches without internet is a separate question you have to check. Store descriptions mix the two up all the time, so if you want to play somewhere with no signal, picking from the description alone is easy to get wrong.

Search phrases split the same way. In English, "best offline idle tycoon games" and "idle games with offline progress" both show up in autocomplete, and in Indonesian people type a game name into a question like "apakah game idle theme park tycoon offline." The table below answers that question with what the stores say.

With the tag: Idle Miner Tycoon, Egg, Inc., Idle Bank Tycoon

Idle Miner Tycoon is the biggest game in this table, with 100M+ installs and about 5.86 million reviews at a 4.5 rating. Its description repeats the line about running your mine offline. That line alone cannot tell you whether it means the game launches without internet or that money piles up while the app is closed.

Egg, Inc. has 10M+ installs and a 4.7 rating. Its description has no offline wording, yet the Google Play tag is there. Idle Bank Tycoon calls itself an "offline bank simulator" and says staff earn cash even while you are offline. All three are free, with ads and in-app purchases.

Says it needs internet: AdVenture Capitalist

AdVenture Capitalist has 10M+ installs and about 1.64 million reviews at a 4.5 rating, and it is one of the originals of the genre. The description says you can earn offline by automating, but the same description also says "an internet connection is required to play." The Google Play "Offline" tag is missing too. If you want something for a flight or a subway ride, this one is out, going by its own description.

Pay once and done: Game Dev Story, Game Dev Tycoon

Strictly speaking these are slow-paced management games rather than idle games, but people searching for offline play tend to look at them too. Game Dev Story is $4.49 on Google Play ($4.99 on the App Store), carries the "Offline" tag, and has 1M+ installs at a 4.7 rating. Game Dev Tycoon is $4.99, its store description says no ads and no in-app purchases, and on Steam it is $9.99 for Windows, Mac and Linux. We did not find offline wording or a tag for it.

What sets both apart from free idle games is that you pay once and play through to the end without ads.

Offline progress, but no tag: Idle Theme Park Tycoon

Idle Theme Park Tycoon has 10M+ installs and a 4.1 rating, and its feature list includes "offline progress." The description says money builds up even when you are not playing, but there is no Google Play "Offline" tag. If you ask whether this game works offline, as in that Indonesian search phrase, the accurate answer by store labels is "progress continues while it is closed; whether it launches without internet is not stated."

PC Cafe Tycoon: what it does well and where it falls short

PC Cafe Tycoon, made by Dumb Game Lab, is an idle management game where you inherit a back-alley PC cafe and grow it into the best in the country. The store description says it can be played offline, and once you hire part-timers the shop keeps running while the app is closed. You open with 6 seats, and ramen stations and vending machines sell better the closer they sit to the seats. A day lasts 2 minutes and speed goes up to 3x.

The weak points are clear. It is a new release with 100+ installs on Google Play and no rating yet, and unlike some games in the table above it has no Google Play "Offline" tag. It has 6 stages from the alley to nationwide, so it has an end, and it does not offer the long run or the depth of Idle Miner Tycoon or Game Dev Tycoon. Progress is saved on the device only, so you need your own plan for moving it when you switch phones. Watching an ad at payout doubles that day's earnings, and the game moves on if you skip the ad.

Which one fits you

If you plan to play where there is no signal, start with the games that have both the Google Play tag and the store wording, and finish by launching the game once in airplane mode. That is the surest check. The labels in this post can change whenever a store description changes.

  • Big idle games to run for a long time: Idle Miner Tycoon, Egg, Inc.
  • A banking theme: Idle Bank Tycoon
  • An idle game that says it needs internet: AdVenture Capitalist (skip it for offline use)
  • Buy once and play to the end: Game Dev Story, Game Dev Tycoon
  • A short phone idle game with offline wording: PC Cafe Tycoon

FAQ

Do idle tycoon games work offline?

It depends on the game. Idle Miner Tycoon, Egg, Inc., Idle Bank Tycoon and Game Dev Story carry the Google Play "Offline" tag, while the AdVenture Capitalist store description says an internet connection is required.

Do idle games keep earning money while the app is closed?

The store descriptions of Idle Theme Park Tycoon, Idle Bank Tycoon and PC Cafe Tycoon say progress continues while the app is closed. That wording does not mean the game launches without internet.

What are good tycoon games to play without Wi-Fi?

Start with the ones carrying the Google Play "Offline" tag: Idle Miner Tycoon, Egg, Inc., Idle Bank Tycoon and Game Dev Story. Before you rely on one, launch it once in airplane mode to be sure.

Is there a tycoon game you buy once, without ads?

Game Dev Story is $4.49 on Google Play and Game Dev Tycoon is $4.99. The Game Dev Tycoon store description says it has no ads and no in-app purchases.
