Fact #12

In Antarctica, Even the Time Zone Follows the Delivery Guy

At the very bottom of the Earth, the South Pole station's clocks are set to New Zealand time

Justin Lee, Dumb Game Lab · 2026-10-24

Short answer The South Pole station runs on New Zealand time, because that's where its supplies are sent from.

The very bottom of the Earth: the South Pole. Every line of longitude meets at this one point.

Time zones are divided up by longitude. So what time is it where all the lines of longitude meet?

Wouldn't every time be right?

The South Pole is home to America's Amundsen-Scott South Pole Station. This station uses New Zealand time.

That's because the base that sends it supplies is in Christchurch, New Zealand.

Other stations in Antarctica are similar. They use the time of the country that claims the land or the country that sends them supplies.

So Antarctica has lots of time zones all mixed together.
