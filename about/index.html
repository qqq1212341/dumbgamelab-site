About the studio

One person.
Played in 119 countries.

Dumb Game Lab is an independent game studio in Korea, run by one developer after work and on weekends. Every game starts from an everyday place — the neighborhood PC cafe, the bathhouse, the corner bakery — and gets polished until it feels good in your hand.

Who makes these

Made after hours

I have been making small games for five years, in the evenings after work and on weekends. Dumb Game Lab is where they finally ship.

Concept, art direction, code, sound and localization are done in-house. Every game launches in eight languages, because the corner PC cafe exists in Taipei, Hanoi and Jakarta too.

— Justin Lee, Founder
