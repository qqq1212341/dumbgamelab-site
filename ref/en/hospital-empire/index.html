The short answer

The 10 minute ad boost gives +50% income, and the 12,000 won No Ads Pack sells the same +50% for good plus ad removal. About 45 minutes of play showed no forced ads. Every ad was one you chose, and the one reward ad we timed ran over 90 seconds. The pack pops up each time you come back, and the vault is capped at 1,000 coins at the start. One treatment room paid 591 about every 26 seconds. We paid nothing.

FAQ

What is in the Hospital Empire Tycoon No Ads Pack?

On the screen it reads -70%, 12,000 won, income +50%, no ads and +1,000 gems, with a 23 hour 28 minute timer. The +50% is the same number the 10 minute ad boost gives for free. We did not buy it.

How long does the ad boost last in Hospital Empire Tycoon?

Each ad adds 10 minutes of +50% income, and the screen caps the total at 1 hour, so six ads fill it.

How fast does the first treatment room pay in Hospital Empire Tycoon?

+591 about every 26 seconds, which is roughly 1,360 a minute, against 26 paid out in salaries about every 57 seconds. The first upgrade costs 20 and the price rises about 1% a level.

Does Hospital Empire Tycoon force ads on you?

Not in about 45 minutes of play: no full-screen or banner ads. The ads we saw were ones we tapped. The reward ad we timed ran over 90 seconds and ended on an install button. The No Ads Pack pop-up comes back every time you return to the game.

How we measured

We played about 45 minutes on a Galaxy Z Flip5 held closed, so the game ran on the outer 748x720 screen, and recorded four segments. The numbers were re-checked against those recordings on 13 Nov 2026. We paid nothing.

The game uses the same code base as the developer's lawyer-office tycoon, with a hospital skin on top.
