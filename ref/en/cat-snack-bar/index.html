The short answer

The ads are everywhere and most of them hand you something. We counted 48 rewarded ad spot types in the app's code, plus a full-screen ad that starts from store 4 and pays 1 gem and 30 gold for watching. A daily ticket skips 10 rewarded ads from store 18, and it recharges at 09:00 so it also gives you a reason to come back. Offline pay stops at 3 hours, and an ad multiplies it by 8. The first store is over in four upgrades that cost 5, 25, 50 and 100 coins. We watched no ad and paid nothing.

FAQ

How many rewarded ad spots does Cat Snack Bar have?

48 types in the app's code. The always-on ones run on timers: a Lucky revenue offer every 60 seconds, a helper bubble every 45, a speed-up bubble every 32 and an income x2 booster for 600 seconds.

What does the ad-skip ticket do in Cat Snack Bar?

It skips 10 rewarded ads a day. It opens at store 18 and recharges at 09:00 every day, with a push saying so. Buying a no-ads product also removes the full-screen ads.

How much offline pay does Cat Snack Bar give?

It starts after 180 seconds away and stops at 10,800 seconds, 3 hours. The base rate is about 30% of working income by our reading, and an ad makes it 8 times larger.

What does the first upgrade cost in Cat Snack Bar?

5 coins for an extra part-timer. Store 1 has only four upgrades, 5, 25, 50 and 100 coins, so it ends in a few minutes. We saw the same four prices on our phone.

How many stores are there in Cat Snack Bar?

765 store rows in the data, 728 regular and 37 for events, grouped into 104 chapters. You move to a new store instead of resetting, and the early stores hold 4, 10, 24, 41 and 50 upgrades.

How we measured

Most numbers were read from the game's own data files (the Android build, version 1.0.219, analysed on 2026-11-15). On 2026-11-16 we installed it on a Galaxy Z Flip5 and wrote down the first screens. Rows marked Measured come from the files, rows marked Seen on screen come from the phone.

We paid nothing and watched no ad. Method code was not readable, so reward amounts and daily caps come partly from constant names.
