Brawl Stars shop prices & gem pack math

Measured 2026-11-12 on Galaxy Z Flip5 (SM-F731N, 2640x1080 landscape). App version 70.757.

The short answer

A 699-gem skin on sale cannot be covered by any gem pack under 950 gems. That pack is 79,000 won. Two 360-gem packs reach 720 gems for 60,000 won by our arithmetic. Gem packs run from 30 gems for 3,000 won to 2,000 gems for 149,000 won, and the per-gem price falls from 100 won to 74.5 won. Brawl Pass is 14,000 won. We bought nothing and did not play a match.

FAQ

How many won does a 699-gem skin cost in Brawl Stars?

The smallest single pack that covers 699 gems is the 950-gem pack at 79,000 won. Two 360-gem packs give 720 gems for 60,000 won by our arithmetic. Our account already held 10 gems. We did not buy the skin.

Which Brawl Stars gem pack is the best value?

The 2,000-gem pack at 149,000 won, tagged Best value: 74.5 won per gem against 100 won for the 30-gem pack. The 950 pack is 83.2 and the 360 pack 83.3, almost the same.

How much is Brawl Pass in Brawl Stars?

14,000 won, the first tile in the shop at version 70.757. The same page lists three special bundles at 23,000, 15,000 and 39,000 won with 24 days 2 hours left.

Can you buy coins with gems in Brawl Stars?

Yes. 300 coins cost 30 gems, 880 cost 80, 2,040 cost 170 and 4,680 cost 360. The bigger packs add a bonus: 80, 340 and 1,080 coins on top.

How we measured

We read the shop on a Galaxy Z Flip5 in landscape, on an account that was four days old with 101 trophies. A 134 second recording and eight screen captures were taken. We paid nothing and left the sound off.

One spray card was tapped by mistake and a purchase confirmation opened. We backed out, and the gem count stayed at 10.
