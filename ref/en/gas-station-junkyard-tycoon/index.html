The short answer

This one is a walk-around game with idle help, and its money curve is hand-tuned, not a formula. The first three pumps cost 5, 50 and 130. Upgrading a pump doubles its income, 10 to 20 per car. Cars come every 11 seconds, then every 5 once the third pump stands. Forced ads start after 180 seconds and repeat every 180, and a rewarded ad buys you 180 seconds of quiet. Offline pay stops at 2 hours. The ad-free pack comes with 1,350 soft coins, 135 iron bars and 80 gems.

FAQ

How much do the first pumps cost in Junkyard Tycoon?

The first pump costs 5, the second 50 and the third 130 soft coins. Upgrading them to level 2 costs 305, 390 and 475, and hiring a pump worker costs 215.

How often do forced ads show up?

The app's defaults are a first ad at 180 seconds and then one every 180 seconds. After you watch a rewarded ad there is a 180-second break. Banners are off.

What does the ad-free pack include?

Ads off plus 1,350 soft coins, 135 iron bars and 80 gems. Rewarded ads stay in the game.

How fast do cars arrive?

One car every 11 seconds at the start and one every 5 seconds once the third pump is built.

How many hours of offline earnings do you get?

Up to 7,200 seconds per map, which is 2 hours. Watching an ad doubles the claim.
