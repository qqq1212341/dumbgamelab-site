Subway Surfers game-over ads, Remove Ads price & shop

undefined

The short answer

No ad interrupted us in four game overs, about four minutes. The ad sits on the button that lets you keep running: Continue carries an ad icon. Remove Ads is 3,000 won and bundles boosters, boards and keys. A separate ad ticket pack, 10 for 4,400 won, lets you take ad rewards without watching. We bought nothing, so we cannot say what Remove Ads takes away.

FAQ

Does Subway Surfers show an ad when you crash?

In our four game overs a full-screen ad never forced its way in. The ad is an offer: the Continue button has an ad icon and a "3x bonus" tag. We did not tap it, so we do not know what a continue ad is like.

How much is Remove Ads in Subway Surfers?

3,000 won on the Korean store. The listing also says boosters +10, boards +20 and keys +15. We did not buy it, so we cannot say which ads it removes.

What are ad tickets in Subway Surfers?

A separate item that says you can take the reward right away without watching the ad: 10 for 4,400 won, 25 for 7,500 won, 60 for 15,000 won. That is 440 won a ticket at the small pack and 250 at the big one, by our arithmetic. We did not spend one.

How many keys can you buy in Subway Surfers, and for how much?

Five packs: 25 for 7,500 won, 55 for 15,000, 125 for 30,000, 350 for 79,000 and 800 for 149,000. The 55 pack is tagged Most popular. A new account starts with 5 keys.

How we measured

We installed the game fresh from the Play Store on a Galaxy Z Flip5 and recorded two sessions at 540x1320, plus screen captures of the shop. We paid nothing, did not tap ad or install buttons and kept the sound off.

The age slider was set to 18.
