The short answer

A tower defense where every wave ends with a pick of one card out of three, and the match freezes while you choose. A wave took us 25 to 30 seconds, so a 20-wave match is about 10 minutes. We met no ad in the tutorial or the first chapter. The money sits outside the match: the first chest takes 3 hours to open, and the upgrade price doubles from 1,000 to 2,000 coins. We never opened the shop, the passes or the later chapters, so their prices are not measured.

FAQ

How long does one match of Raid Rush take?

About 10 minutes for a full 20-wave match. One wave took us 25 to 30 seconds: around 10 s to summon, 12 to 15 s of fighting and 3 to 5 s to pick a card. The 7-wave tutorial is much shorter.

Does Raid Rush show ads during a match?

Not in what we played: 0 ads in about 23 minutes on 30 Oct and about 8 minutes on 2 Nov, both inside the tutorial and the first chapter. Reviews on Play do complain about ads, so later chapters may differ. We did not get that far.

How much does it cost to upgrade a tower in Raid Rush?

1,000 coins for level 1 to 2 and 2,000 for level 2 to 3, plus one blueprint of that tower each time. The card shows a cap of level 10. A tutorial chest holds 2,000 coins, which is exactly the level 2 to 3 step.

How long is the chest timer in Raid Rush?

The first chest showed a 3-hour unlock. It held 2,000 coins, one tower blueprint and one skill blueprint, and the home screen has 4 chest slots. We did not test paying gems to skip the wait.

How we measured

We installed the Play build on a Galaxy Z Flip5, played the 7-wave tutorial and the early waves of chapter 1 by hand, and recorded the screen. Counts and sizes were read from captures and frames.

On 2 Nov we reinstalled on a new account to recheck the starting wallet, the chest and the upgrade prices. We paid nothing. The app files were unpacked to read class and variable names, which is where the ad mediator list comes from.

How we check games

What we did not measure

  • Anything past the first chapter: bosses, air units, later chapters and the hard mode.
  • The shop, battle pass and pack prices, which we did not open.
  • A second free refresh, revive and any rewarded ad.
  • Enemy health, wave tables and drop rates, which sit in config files we could not read.
