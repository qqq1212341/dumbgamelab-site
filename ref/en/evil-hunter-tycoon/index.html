The short answer

We found 7 rewarded ad spots and no text for full-screen ads. Two of the spots walk around the map: an ad goblin that lets you pick gems or gold, and a treasure chest that gives gold or a 1-hour EXP buff. Instead of removing ads, the shop sells skips that keep the reward: 100 skip tickets for 4,900 or 30 days of skips for 11,000 (price column, read as KRW). There are 62 novice quests, and their gold reward climbs 200, 250, 300, 400 as the town hall levels up.

FAQ

Does Evil Hunter Tycoon have forced full-screen ads?

We found no UI text for them. All ad text is for rewarded ads: 7 spots that pay gems, gold, buffs, hunters or elementals.

Can you remove ads?

You skip them and keep the reward: 100 skip tickets cost 4,900 and 30 days of skips cost 11,000 in the shop table.

How many novice quests are there?

62. The last one asks you to switch to Normal difficulty.
