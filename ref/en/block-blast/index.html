The short answer

No ads for the first twelve minutes: no banner, no popup, no ad offer. After that, every game over we hit played a full-screen video, three out of three, at about 31, 30 and 42 seconds. The first had no skip button and the third had a skip arrow. We found no shop and no remove-ads button in the settings or on the home screen.

FAQ

When does Block Blast show its first ad?

At the first game over, about 12 minutes in. Before that, through the tutorial and the whole first game, we saw no banner, popup or ad offer.

How long is an ad in Block Blast?

We timed three: about 31, 30 and 42 seconds. A 50-second game was followed by a 30-second ad, and a 124-second game by a 42-second one, so even a short game pays the full ad.

Can you skip the ads in Block Blast?

Not the first one: it showed a speaker icon and nothing else. The third had a skip arrow. All three ended on a card that we closed with Close or an X.

Is there a remove-ads button in Block Blast?

We did not find one. The settings menu has sound, music, vibration, More Games, Home, Replay and Default Skin, and the home screen has no shop. We did not read the store's in-app purchase list, so a purchase may exist outside the game screens.

How we measured
