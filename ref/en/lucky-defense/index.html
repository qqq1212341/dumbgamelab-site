Lucky Defense ads, prices & drop rates

Measured 2026-11-04 on Galaxy Z Flip5 (1080x2640). App version 2.0.17.

The short answer

Luck runs the matches, and the odds are printed on screen. A legendary hero is 0.45% per summon at the base odds level and 1.45% at level 6, and each odds upgrade cost a flat 100 coins in our matches. We saw 0 ads through the first ten waves. Shop gem packs run from 5,500 to 150,000 won. We bought nothing and did not play PvP, so we can't say whether paying wins fights.

FAQ

Is Lucky Defense pay to win?

We only measured the match side, not PvP. In a match, the odds of better heroes go up with coins you earn there, 100 per step, not with cash. Cash goes to the shop: gem packs from 5,500 to 150,000 won and a 6,600 won starter pack. Whether gems win fights against other players, we have not tested.

Can you play Lucky Defense without ads?

At the start, yes. The tutorial match and waves 1 to 10 had 0 ads in two sessions. The shop does have ad buttons, one on the reset slot with a 2/2 counter and one on relic find. We did not test later modes, and we did not buy the VIP pass that is said to remove ads.

What are the drop rates in Lucky Defense?

In-match summons at the base odds level: common 90.09%, rare 7.21%, epic 2.25%, legendary 0.45%. At level 6 it is 69.16, 22.13, 7.26 and 1.45. The roulette is 60% blue, 20% purple and 10% gold. Hero recruit odds sit behind an odds info button that we did not capture, and mythic heroes are built from recipes instead of rolled.

How much does a summon cost in Lucky Defense?

The first summon is 20 coins and every summon adds 2, so it was 56 after 18 summons. Merging heroes did not bring it back down.

What we did not measure

  • Waves 11 to 80 enemy HP.
  • Hero recruit, pet and rune odds (behind info buttons we did not open).
  • Ad rewards, VIP and pass prices, and co-op matchmaking.
  • Sound and haptics.

How we measured

We installed the Korean-store build on a Galaxy Z Flip5 on three separate days and played it with a new account. Numbers marked "Seen on screen" were read from screenshots taken at full 1080x2640. Times are from screen recordings.

We paid nothing and did not watch ads to the end. The tutorial match was played twice.
