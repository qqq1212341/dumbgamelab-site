The short answer

Money first, ads barely. The app files hold 2,856 priced products across ten price tiers, and most of them sit at $4.99 and above: $99.99 has 624 products and $19.99 has 619. A top-rarity (UR) hero is 1% per recruit with a guaranteed one at 50 recruits, or 200 if you pick the hero. Rewarded ads are only four slots at 5 plays a day each, and there is a permanent ad-removal product. The early game on our phone is a count-the-gates runner, not the base builder the store art shows. We did not buy anything, so what a paid pack actually delivers is unmeasured.

FAQ

What are the odds of a UR hero in Last War?

1% per recruit, against 4% for SSR and 2% for SR. The other 93% is fragments and filler. A UR is guaranteed at 50 recruits, or at 200 if you pick a wish hero. We read this from the recruit tables and the game's text, not from our own pulls.

How much do Last War purchases cost?

Ten price tiers, from $0.99 to $99.99. Of 2,856 priced products, 624 are $99.99 and 619 are $19.99, while only 32 are $0.99. A weekly pass is $4.99 and a monthly pass is $24.99.

Does Last War have ads?

Only a little. The files list four rewarded-ad slots (resources, hero recruit, survivor recruit, trade contract) at 5 plays a day each, switched on in late November 2025, and a product that removes ads for good. We did not see an ad on our phone.

Is the gate-running game from the ads really in Last War?

Yes. The tables hold 110 count-the-gates stages in 11 chapters, called Special Ops, and our phone opened on one. Later chapters unlock as the headquarters level rises, and the main game is a base builder with alliances and servers.

How long does it take to upgrade the headquarters?

The HQ goes to level 35. Reading the build-time column as seconds, level 10 is about 7.4 hours and level 30 about 112 days before speed-ups. One hour of build speed-up costs 180 diamonds, about $1.80.
