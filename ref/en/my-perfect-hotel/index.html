The short answer

The ads are quiet for seven minutes and the sales pitch is not. A 15,000 won welcome pack with permanent ad removal appears under a minute into the recording, and an ad-free notice comes at about seven minutes. Close the pack and a full-screen ad follows within 8 seconds. The playable ad that followed had no close button for 2 minutes 36 seconds. The three ad-free packs cost 15,000, 22,000 and 37,000 won. We paid nothing.

FAQ

What does the first pop-up in My Perfect Hotel sell?

A Welcome Pack for 15,000 won. Day 1 has permanent ad removal, a 10% income bonus and 350 gems, with more rewards over three days. It showed up about 45 seconds into our recording, less than 2 minutes after launch.

Can you close the playable ad in My Perfect Hotel?

Not in our runs. One playable ad showed no close button for 156 seconds of waiting. The Back key did nothing and 25 more seconds of touching the screen found no X. We never saw how that ad ends.

How much does ad removal cost in My Perfect Hotel?

The three packs that include it are 15,000, 22,000 and 37,000 won. The settings screen has no remove-ads button of its own, so it is only sold inside these packs as far as we saw.

What happens when you close the ad-free pack in My Perfect Hotel?

A full-screen ad came within 1.5 to 8 seconds, both times we tried it. First a MapleStory idle game card, then a Last Asylum playable ad. The "Not interested?" box shows a 2 day 23 hour timer before that.

How we measured

We installed the game fresh from the Play Store on a Galaxy Z Flip5 and recorded six segments at 540x1320. We paid nothing, kept the sound off and did not tap the Install or Play Now buttons inside ads.

The app was deleted after measuring.

How we check games

What we did not measure

  • How a playable ad ends, and when its X appears.
  • Whether the game offers a rewarded ad the player can choose.
  • A daily ad cap, and how often ads come after the 7 minute notice.
  • What the packs give after you buy them.
