The short answer

You tap to collect cash at first, and the game sells you the end of that tapping. Auto-collect costs 8, 12 and 16 gems for the three starting stations, and the first chapter pays 5 gems, so the first purchase sits just out of reach. Offline earnings stop at 2 hours. A $4.99 offer removes ads and a $7.99 pass doubles income for good. The game's own files hold 263 store products. We paid for none of them.

FAQ

How long does a car stay at each station in Roadside Empire?

On the first road a car spends 4 seconds at the gas station, 10 at the car wash and 15 at the toilets, in that order. On later roads the times step up by 5 seconds per station, up to 40.

How much does auto-collect cost?

8 gems for the gas station, 12 for the car wash and 16 for the toilets. The first chapter pays 5 gems.

How long do offline earnings last?

Up to 7,200 seconds, which is 2 hours. You need to be away at least 300 seconds to get a payout. A rewarded ad adds more on return.

What does the 20-minute income boost cost?

x2 for 20 minutes is free if you watch an ad, 10 gems without one, and 250 gems in the shop. x3 for 20 minutes is 800 gems.

Can you pay to remove the ads?

The product list has an ad removal offer at $4.99 and an ad ticket subscription at $5.99 or $8.99. The permanent x2 income pass is $7.99.
