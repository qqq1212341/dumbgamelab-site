The short answer

Light on ads for the first quarter hour. In 1,189 seconds of recording the game never forced an ad. The only ad offer, a x2 booster for 5 minutes, showed up about 14 minutes in, after every upgrade on the menu was bought. The iOS store lists a Permanent Boost at $5.39. It is a single-player idle game, so we saw nothing to pay-to-win against.

FAQ

How long until Eatventure shows an ad?

About 14 minutes. The first ad offer, a Booster x2 for 5 minutes, showed up at 14:14 after we had bought every item on the upgrade menu. In 1,189 seconds of recording we saw no forced full-screen ad.

Can you play Eatventure without ads?

In the first 15 minutes, yes. Nothing interrupted us, and the one ad offer is a button you choose to press. We did not test later hours, events or what the paid options remove.

Is Eatventure pay to win?

There is nothing to win against: we only saw a single-player idle game. What money buys is speed. The iOS US store lists a Permanent Boost at $5.39, gem bundles from $2.19 to $10.99 and event offers up to $26.99. In 15 minutes we hit the remodel gate without paying.

How much does the first remodel cost in Eatventure?

The card asks for the lemonade station at level 25 and 847 coins. We reached level 25 with 727 coins at 15:48, so the last 120 coins were the wait.
