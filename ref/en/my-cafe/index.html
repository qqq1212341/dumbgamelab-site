My Cafe diamond pack prices, staff costs & the first 14 minutes

undefined

The short answer

No ads in the first 870 seconds: no forced ad, no starter-pack popup, no rewarded-ad offer, and no energy bar. The money sits in the diamond shop instead. Six packs run from 3,000 won for 200 diamonds to 150,000 won for 14,000, and the staff slots cost 10, 75 and 499 diamonds. Coins are small early on: a cup of tea pays 4.

FAQ

What is the most expensive diamond pack in My Cafe?

150,000 won for 14,000 diamonds, with a +40% bonus tag. That works out to about 10.7 won per diamond, while the 200 and 500 packs cost 15 won each. There are six packs in all, from 3,000 won.

Does My Cafe have ads?

Not in the first 870 seconds. We saw no forced ad, no starter-pack popup and no rewarded-ad offer, because the booster, reward and calendar menus were still locked. The store page does say the app contains ads.

How much does it cost to hire staff in My Cafe?

The two candidates we saw cost 30 coins (Ann) and 50 coins (Dean). The harder part is the slot: the three locked slots unlock at levels 5, 8 and 10 for 10, 75 and 499 diamonds. A new candidate list costs 20 diamonds or arrives after 24 hours.

How fast do you level up in My Cafe?

We reached level 1 at about 290 seconds, level 2 at 404 and level 3 at about 795. The rewards were 80, 100 and 250 coins. Level 1 needed 200 stars.

Does My Cafe make you wait with timers or energy?

There is no energy bar. We also never saw a waiting timer, so we could not find what a speed-up costs in gems. The only timer we met was the 24-hour refresh on the staff candidate list.

What we did not measure

  • Anything after 870 seconds: events, the season pass and the rewarded-ad offers that unlock later.
  • Whether a coin purchase exists anywhere (the bank had diamonds only).
  • The gift, purple and booster packs, which were locked at levels 5, 9 and 8.
  • Sound.

How we measured

We installed the game from the Play Store on a Galaxy Z Flip5 and played about 14 minutes by hand (870 seconds on the recording clock). The 2026.11.0.0 version number comes from the APK file we took apart for the file-level notes, not from the Play build we played.

We paid nothing, pressed no Watch button on any ad prompt and deleted the app afterwards.
