Mobile game references, measured on a phone

Ad spots, price lists, drop rates and economy numbers from popular mobile games. Every number was read or timed on our own phone, with our own screenshots.

We install popular mobile games on a test phone and write down what the screens show: where the ads are, what the shop charges, what the odds are. Each page says when we measured, on what, and which numbers are only read from the store listing.
