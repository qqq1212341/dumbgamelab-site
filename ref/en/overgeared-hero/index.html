The short answer

The money is in the backpack. The free bag has 26 slots, the next two cost 3,500 and 7,000 gems, and the full 48-slot bag is 17,000 won in cash with no gem option. Gem packs run from 1,500 to 149,000 won, and the remove-ads card is 14,000 won. We saw five ad spots, all optional, and each one opens an offer for that card first. Energy gives 1 point per 12 minutes and a run costs 5. We paid nothing, so we did not see the game after any purchase.

FAQ

How much is the largest backpack in Overgeared Hero?

The 48-slot Legendary bag costs 17,000 won in cash, and there is no gem price for it. The 32-slot bag is 3,500 gems and the 38-slot bag is 7,000 gems. Bags are bought per class, and the free bag has 26 slots.

How fast does energy come back in Overgeared Hero?

One point per 12 minutes, up to 35. A run costs 5, so one hour of waiting pays for one run. An early exit gives some back: 2 after losing in round 4 and 1 after giving up in round 6. The monthly card adds 20 to the cap.

Where do the ads show up in Overgeared Hero?

We found five spots, all optional: the plus-one coin button, two free rerolls per run, one revive per run, double speed per chapter, and 110 gold twice a day. We saw no forced interstitials. Each button first opens the 14,000-won remove-ads card.

What does the 9,900 won monthly card give in Overgeared Hero?

For 30 days: 4x battle speed, one more daily dungeon run, 20% more expedition reward, unlimited quick expeditions, and 20 more max energy. It also pays 800 gems at purchase and 400 gems a day. That is what the card screen lists; we did not buy it.

How we measured

We played the Korean-store build on a Galaxy Z Flip5 on two days, 2026-10-26 and 2026-11-15. The first session ran about two hours at full 1080x2640. In the second we re-read the money screens on the outer display (748x720) on version 1.43.1.

We paid nothing and left the app deleted afterwards. Numbers marked "Seen on screen" come from screenshots. Times come from screen recordings.
