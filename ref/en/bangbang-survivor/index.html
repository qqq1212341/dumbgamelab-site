The short answer

A vertical survival shooter where the hero fires on its own and every level-up is a pick of one skill out of three. The first 20-wave stage ended at 5:18, and a 1,500 won starter offer popped up right after the clear. Gem packs run from 1,500 to 149,000 won, and we counted five ad buttons in about 9 minutes: skill reset, double reward, free chest pull, free gold and free stamina. We paid nothing, and the damage numbers were too small to read in our recording.

FAQ

How long is the first stage of BangBang Survivor?

20 waves, and our first clear came at 5:18. Wave 1 had 3 to 5 zombies at 0:12, wave 10 had 30 to 50 at 2:20, and by waves 17 to 20 the screen held over 100. A level-up pick comes every 20 to 30 seconds.

When does BangBang Survivor ask you to pay for the first time?

Right after the first clear, about 5 minutes in. A 1,500 won starter gift opens with a three-day reward list. It is the smallest gem pack price too, which runs up to 149,000 won.

What do chest pulls cost in BangBang Survivor?

The plain chest is 50 gems a pull or 500 for ten. The shiny chest is 250 or 2,500 for ten, with a guaranteed prize promised on the tenth pull. Each has one free pull by ad. Drop rates were not shown on the screens we saw.

How many ad buttons does BangBang Survivor have?

Five places in about 9 minutes: skill reset on a level-up, 2x reward on the win screen, a free pull on the treasure tab, 500 free gold in the gold shop and 6 stamina in the daily deals. We did not press any.

How we measured

We installed version 1.69 from APKPure on a Galaxy Z Flip5 and recorded the screen for four stretches, about 9 minutes, plus screenshots. The UI was in Korean.

We paid nothing and pressed no ad button. Times and counts were read from the frames, so zombie numbers are estimates by eye.
