The short answer

The free part is a long wait and the paid part is a shortcut. Six of the 14 maps cost money, and you get one free key every 7 days to open one of them. On our phone the key timer read 6 days 23 hours 59 minutes on the first screen, next to a daily reward table whose day 3 and day 7 pay 1 and 5 gems. One reward-ad offer can appear every 60 seconds, so the ad buttons keep coming back. The store reviews we read complain about both the ads and the 6-day wait. We paid nothing and watched no ad.

FAQ

How many maps are there in Obby Parkour and which ones cost money?

14 maps. Six are paid: Tower of Hell and Hide and Seek (12 each), Red green light (25), Prison (55), Climb and jump and Find the cats (10 each). The price field does not name the currency.

How does the free key work in Obby Parkour?

You get 1 key every 7 days and it opens 1 paid map. Our phone showed key 0/1 with a 6d 23h 59m timer on the lobby, and a key timer on the paid map card. One store review complains about the 6-day wait.

What does the daily reward pay in Obby Parkour?

Day 1 to 7: 100 coins, 100, 1 gem, 150, 150, 200, 5 gems. CLAIM x2 asks for an ad, and CLAIM EVERYTHING AT ONCE is a paid product.

How often can Obby Parkour offer a rewarded ad?

A global timer of 60 seconds sits between offers, and it keeps counting while you are away. Spots include the main-screen free skin, the daily double, the map-finish reward and revives. We watched none.

How much do gems cost in Obby Parkour?

From the price field: 2 gems $2, 4 gems $3, 10 gems $5, 20 gems $9. That is $1 a gem at the bottom and 45 cents at the top. Store prices can differ by country.
