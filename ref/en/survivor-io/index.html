The short answer

The energy bar is the product. It holds 60, gives back 1 every 10 minutes, and each run costs 5 even if you die in 18 seconds. A free refill by ad is 5 energy, three times a day. Seven ad networks are in the app, yet in 7 runs we met no forced ad. The shop stayed locked for the first 6 minutes of survival, which we never reached.

FAQ

How much energy does one run of Survivor.io cost?

5, and it is spent even if you lose. The bar holds 60 and refills 1 per 10 minutes, so 5 is 50 minutes of waiting. Seven runs in a row took us from 60 to about 25.

How do you get free energy in Survivor.io?

Watching an ad in the Buy Energy popup gives 5, three times a day. Adventure level-ups also paid 5 energy at levels 2 and 3. We did not see a gem price for energy.

How many ad networks does Survivor.io use?

Seven in the app files: AppLovin MAX as the mediator, plus AdMob, ironSource, Unity Ads, Vungle, Pangle and Mintegral. In seven runs we met no forced ad.

What do you get for losing a run in Survivor.io?

2,500 gold and 1,000 EXP in each of our six lost runs, the same at 18 seconds and at 2:11. That gold goes straight into the 100 and 200 gold nodes of the permanent upgrade tree.

How we measured

We pulled the Play build (5.3.1) off a Galaxy Z Flip5 and read the file strings for the ad networks and product IDs. The in-game config tables are encrypted and we did not break them, so only their names were read.

We then played about 35 minutes by hand, 7 runs and the lobby, on a guest account. We paid nothing and did not watch ads.

How we check games

What we did not measure

  • Anything after 6 minutes of survival: the shop, bosses and weapon evolutions.
  • Prices of in-app products, passes and the monthly card.
  • What the energy ad plays, and revive or double-reward ads.
  • Wave and enemy numbers, which are in encrypted tables.
  • Sound.
