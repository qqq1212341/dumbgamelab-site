The short answer

A game built to sell time. The code lists 32 rewarded-video spots, and the premium currency runs on one rule: 200 Super Cash is about $1. A x2 boost costs 40 Super Cash for an hour, a x100 boost costs 6,000, and 10 days of instant cash costs 1,200. The first rewarded-ad slot, Ad Boost, opens at Shaft 3 of the first mine. On our phone we only got as far as the age slider and the first chapter quest, so we have not seen a single ad play. The ad boost size is not in the files either.

FAQ

How many ad spots does Idle Miner Tycoon have?

The app files list 32 rewarded-video types and one interstitial type. One of the 32 is a debug placeholder. They sit on idle cash, boosts, chests, the lucky wheel, the daily reward and the barrier timers. We read this from the files and did not watch the ads play.

When does Idle Miner Tycoon show its first rewarded ad?

The unlock table opens Ad Boost at Shaft 3 of the first mine, which is after the shop (Shaft 2) and before the world map (Shaft 4). We cannot say how many minutes that takes, because our phone session ended at the chapter 1 quest.

How much is a boost in Idle Miner Tycoon?

A x2 boost for one hour is 40 Super Cash, x10 is 560, x50 is 3,000 and x100 is 6,000. At 200 Super Cash for about $1, that puts the x100 hour near $30.

What is the biggest Super Cash pack?

The bundle names in the files run 300, 800, 1,700, 3,600, 9,500 and 20,000 Super Cash. At 200 per dollar the top one is about $100. The Korean Play page lists in-app items from 1,000 to 130,000 won.

Is there a daily login reward in Idle Miner Tycoon?

Yes, a 30-day calendar. Day 1 gives 20 Super Cash, day 7 gives 5 green skill points, day 14 an epic chest and day 30 a legendary chest. An ad can double the reward.

How we measured

Most numbers here were read from the game's own data files (an Android build analysed on 2026-10-16). We then installed the Play Store version on a Galaxy Z Flip5 on 2026-11-07 and wrote down the first screens. Rows marked "Measured" come from the data files, rows marked "Seen on screen" come from the phone.

We paid nothing and watched no ads. Dollar figures use the 200 Super Cash per $1 rate that the item table implies, and they are our conversion.

What we did not measure

  • How big the ad boost really is: the files hold only skill-tree bonuses.
  • Shop prices on screen: the shop did not open in the early tutorial.
  • How long the first ad takes to appear, and what it gives.
  • Battle pass and growth fund reward tables, which the server sends.
  • The data files were read on 2026-10-16 from a build whose version number we did not record. Our phone showed 5.67.0, which Play updated on 2026-10-28.
