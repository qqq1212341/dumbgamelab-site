The short answer

This is a gentle idle game with one ad button. In about 12 minutes we saw no interstitial and no banner. The only ad spot is a lucky box at the bottom of the supply shop. Money comes from tapping acorn bubbles and from a lobby counter that adds 1 acorn per second at the start: 13 to 244 acorns in about 3.9 minutes. The sauna asks for level 20 and 300,000 acorns, which is about 83 hours at that starting rate. We paid nothing and never saw a remove-ads product.

FAQ

Does Animal Hotspring show forced ads?

Not in our test. In about 12 minutes we saw no interstitial and no banner. The only ad is a button you press yourself, the lucky box at the bottom of the supply shop. If you close it early you get nothing. That ad opened another game's store page at 35 seconds.

How long does the sauna take to unlock in Animal Hotspring?

It needs level 20 and 300,000 acorns. With no facilities the lobby gives 1 acorn per second, which would take about 83 hours of waiting. Facilities speed that up, but we did not measure by how much.

How do you get new animals in Animal Hotspring?

Through reputation. Facilities add reputation, for example +15 for a carpet and +50 for a washstand. At 30 reputation two slots open, and you send the owner cat to invite the animals. Later steps need 300, 500, 700, up to 6,900, and the gaps keep growing.

What does a bath supply cost in Animal Hotspring?

From 3 acorns for a sports drink to 100 for a dango. A one-set box is 350 and ten sets are 3,300. Dragging a wanted item onto an animal paid 5 acorns in our run.
