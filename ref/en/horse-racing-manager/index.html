The short answer

A slow, deep manager. The first race starts about 5 minutes after launch and a 6-furlong race runs 1:20 at normal speed. The only ad we actually watched is an always-on button in the shop that pays 3 horseshoes, 2% of the cheapest pack (150 for ₩1,200). Money goes mostly to energy refills and breeding tickets.

FAQ

How long is a race in Horse Racing Manager 2026?

About 80 seconds at normal speed. Our 6-furlong, 12-horse race was won in 1:20.381 and the last horse came in at 1:24.228. Fast forward and Skip are there from the first race.

Does Horse Racing Manager 2026 have a lot of ads?

Not in our first 29 minutes: no forced full-screen ad and no banner. The one video we watched was opt-in, from a button at the top of the shop. It ran about 30 seconds and paid 3 horseshoes.

How much do horseshoes cost?

On Google Play Korea the six packs run from 150 for ₩1,200 to 45K for ₩120,000, all tagged 50% off. A breeding ticket is 150 horseshoes and a full energy refill (30) is 40.

How long until the first race?

About 5 minutes from launch on our phone: sign-in, consent, loading, a TV intro, naming the horse, the stable and the odds board come first.

How we measured

We installed version 9.1.20 on a Galaxy Z Flip5 from the APK because the Play page was not available for that device, then played about 29 minutes by hand and recorded the screen in segments.

Prices were read from the in-game shop on Google Play Korea. We paid nothing and left the sound off. Ad spots we did not see on screen are marked as estimates from the app code.
