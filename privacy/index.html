Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains how Dumb Game Lab ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile games and websites we publish. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location in our games.

1. Information we collect

  • Game data — your progress and settings, stored on your device.
  • Usage data — anonymous gameplay events (for example, level reached or session length) used to fix bugs and balance the game.
  • Device and advertising identifiers — collected by our advertising partner to show and measure ads.
  • Messages you send us — if you email us, we keep your message and email address to reply.

2. Third-party services

Our games use the following services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (anonymous usage analytics) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple App Store and Google Play (purchases) — payments are processed by the stores; we never see your payment details.

3. Advertising and tracking

On iOS, we ask for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any advertising identifier is used for personalized ads. If you decline, you will see non-personalized ads. On Android, you can reset or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings.

4. Children

Our games are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Analytics data is retained by our partners according to their policies. To request deletion of any data linked to you, email contact@dumbgamelab.com.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when posted on this page.

7. Contact

Dumb Game Lab, Seoul, Republic of Korea — contact@dumbgamelab.com
