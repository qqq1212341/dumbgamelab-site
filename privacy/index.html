Privacy Policy

Effective: November 19, 2026 · Previous version: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how Dumb Game Lab handles personal information in its mobile games and websites. We do not ask you to create an account, and our games do not ask for your name, phone number or precise location. The Korean version prevails.

1. What we process and why

  • Game data — progress and settings, stored on your device and not sent to our servers. Games with cloud save keep a copy in your own iCloud (Apple) or Google Play Games account, which we cannot see.
  • Usage data — to fix and balance our games, Google Analytics for Firebase collects play events (stages reached, play time, device model, OS, app version, country) together with a device identifier such as the app instance ID.
  • Advertising identifiers — Google AdMob collects your advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA) and IP address to show and measure ads.
  • Error reports — when a game crashes or hits an error, it sends the error, the recent screen flow, app version, device model and OS. We do not store IP addresses or device IDs.
  • Feedback — text you send with the in-game «Send feedback» button, with the game name, build number and last error, via Google Analytics.
  • Friend invites — invite codes and whether an invite completed. We store only a hash of a random app-generated value; your IP is used only as a daily salted hash to stop abuse. No device or advertising IDs are stored.
  • Purchase records — only when you buy or restore a paid item (section 2).
  • Website visits — dumbgamelab.com uses Google Analytics cookies to record pages visited, referrer, browser and approximate region (section 4).
  • Inquiries — if you email us, we keep your address and message to reply.

2. Purchase records (in-app Premium)

If you buy "Premium" or restore it, our server records the following so we can unlock it, restore it, handle refunds and answer disputes:

  • App Store transaction ID and product ID
  • Purchase, restore and refund date and time
  • App Store country or region, and the environment (production or sandbox)

We do not receive your name or card number; Apple processes the payment. These records are kept for 5 years to handle disputes and meet legal retention rules (Enforcement Decree of the Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, Article 6), then deleted. They are stored on Cloudflare (our server provider). See the Paid Service Policy.

2-1. Pre-registration (Seoul Apocalypse nickname reservation)

  • Collected: nickname (required), email (optional), referral source, and a hash of the connecting IP (the raw IP is not stored).
  • Used for: preventing duplicate nicknames, sending a launch notice, blocking abuse.
  • Retention: until 30 days after launch. If the game is cancelled, deleted immediately. Deletion requests: info@dumbgamelab.com.
  • Stored on: Cloudflare (server provider).

4. Cookies and ad tracking

  • Our website uses Google Analytics cookies. Visitors from the EEA, UK and Switzerland get no analytics or ad cookies by default. You can block or delete cookies in your browser or use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • On iOS we ask for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before using your advertising identifier for personalized ads. If you decline, you see non-personalized ads.
  • On Android you can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in Settings › Google › Ads.

5. Children

Our games are not directed at children under 14 (under 13 in the US) and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn we have, we delete it promptly. Parents can request access or deletion at info@dumbgamelab.com.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the app.
  • Purchase records are kept 5 years, pre-registration data until 30 days after launch, and emails for 1 year after we reply. Analytics data follows section 3.
  • When the period ends or the purpose is met, we delete the data in a way that cannot be recovered.

7. Your rights

You (or your parent or guardian if you are under 14) may request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing, or withdraw consent, at any time by writing to info@dumbgamelab.com. We respond within 10 days. If you are in the EEA or UK you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

All transfers are encrypted (HTTPS). Server access is limited to the representative, and data we don't need, such as IP addresses, is hashed or not stored.
