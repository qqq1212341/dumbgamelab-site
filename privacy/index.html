Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how Dumb Game Lab ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile games and websites we publish. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location in our games.

1. Information we collect

  • Game data — your progress and settings, stored on your device.
  • Usage data — anonymous gameplay events (for example, level reached or session length) used to fix bugs and balance the game.
  • Device and advertising identifiers — collected by our advertising partner to show and measure ads.
  • Purchase records — only if you buy or restore a paid product (see section 2).
  • Messages you send us — if you email us, we keep your message and email address to reply.

2. Purchase records (in-app Premium)

If you buy "Premium" or restore it, our server records the following so we can unlock it, restore it, handle refunds and answer disputes:

  • App Store transaction ID and product ID
  • Purchase, restore and refund date and time
  • App Store country or region, and the environment (production or sandbox)

We do not receive your name or card number; Apple processes the payment. These records are kept for 5 years to handle disputes and meet legal retention rules (Enforcement Decree of the Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, Article 6), then deleted. They are stored on Cloudflare (our server provider). See the Paid Service Policy.

2-1. Pre-registration (Seoul Apocalypse nickname reservation)

  • Collected: nickname (required), email (optional), referral source, and a hash of the connecting IP (the raw IP is not stored).
  • Used for: preventing duplicate nicknames, sending a launch notice, blocking abuse.
  • Retention: until 30 days after launch. If the game is cancelled, deleted immediately. Deletion requests: info@dumbgamelab.com.
  • Stored on: Cloudflare (server provider).

3. Third-party services

Our games use the following services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (anonymous usage analytics) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple App Store and Google Play (purchases) — payments are processed by the stores; we never see your payment details.
  • Cloudflare (server hosting for purchase records) — cloudflare.com/privacypolicy

4. Advertising and tracking

On iOS, we ask for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any advertising identifier is used for personalized ads. If you decline, you will see non-personalized ads. On Android, you can reset or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings.

5. Children

Our games are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Analytics data is retained by our partners according to their policies. Purchase records are kept for 5 years as described above. To request deletion of any data linked to you, email info@dumbgamelab.com; records we must keep by law are deleted when the retention period ends.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when posted on this page.

8. Contact
