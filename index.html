About the studio

Dumb Game Lab started with a simple idea: games don't have to be complicated to be fun. We build small, playful games around everyday places people know — the neighborhood PC cafe, the local bathhouse, the corner store — and polish them until they feel good in your hand.

Every game is made in-house, from the first concept and art to code, sound, localization and live updates. Our games are free to play, supported by optional ads, and available in up to eight languages.

Made in-house

Concept, art, code and sound are all produced by our own team in Seoul.

Short sessions

Play for two minutes or an hour. Idle progress keeps going while you're away.

Live updates

We read every store review and ship fixes and new content regularly.

For everyone

Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Indonesian, Thai and Vietnamese.
