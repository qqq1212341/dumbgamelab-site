The studio

Games don't have to be complicated
to be fun.

Dumb Game Lab is a small independent team in Seoul. We pick an everyday place, find the one thing that's fun about running it, and polish that until it feels good in your hand.

01

Everyday places

PC cafes, bathhouses, corner stores, farms. If you've been there, you already know how to play.

02

Made in-house

Concept, art, code, sound and localization all come out of our own studio.

03

Two minutes or two hours

Short sessions by design. Idle progress keeps going while you're away.

04

Players worldwide

Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified & Traditional), Indonesian, Thai and Vietnamese.
