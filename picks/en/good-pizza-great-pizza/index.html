Games Like Good Pizza, Great Pizza

Written and checked by Justin Lee · Checked 2026-10-30 · Device Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5 (SM-F731N)

We launched eight cooking and shop games to play after Good Pizza, Great Pizza (TapBlaze) on a Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5 on October 30, 2026. Six of the eight reopened past the first screen in airplane mode, while Bakery Story stopped at an internet-required notice and Cooking Diary at its terms window. The benchmark game, tested the same way, got through an age entry window to its tutorial kitchen.

Good Pizza, Great Pizza has you listen to a customer, then build the order yourself, from dough to toppings. Fans tend to split two ways after it. Some stay with hand-made orders, like TapBlaze's own coffee sequel, and others move to idle shops such as Pizza Ready, where staff do the cooking and you just grow the place.

So we sorted the eight picks by who handles the order: you, or the game. Hand-made are Good Coffee, Bakery Story, Cooking Diary and Chichi's Baking. Self-running are Idle Pizza, Pizza Ready, Burger Please! and Chichi's Idle Bakery Tycoon. Whether each one opens with no signal, as the benchmark does, was checked separately.

How we checked

We installed every game on our work phone and took the first-launch and airplane-mode screens ourselves. We did not count ads, so the ad column only copies what each store page labels. The one exception is Idle Pizza, where full-screen ads for another game appeared twice in the first 70 seconds, which we saw. For Good Coffee and Idle Pizza we only reached the title and first screen, so opened means exactly that.

Our two games ran from a build already installed on our phone with a saved game, which can differ from a fresh install and is not the same yardstick as the rest. For Chichi's Baking our taps missed the open-shop button, so we only confirmed the first screen.

FAQ

Does Good Pizza, Great Pizza work without internet?

On our Galaxy Z Flip5 on October 30, 2026, relaunching it in airplane mode got past the age entry window to the tutorial kitchen and the owner's dialogue. We did not play on from the tutorial into a full business day.

Which of these did not open in airplane mode?

Bakery Story showed a notice that the game is online and needs a connection, and went no further. Cooking Diary stopped at its terms window, which we did not accept, so what follows is unchecked. The other six opened past their first screen.

Which games here are closest to the original's hand-made orders?

Good Coffee and Chichi's Baking are the closest, since you build each order yourself. Pizza Ready, Burger Please! and Idle Pizza are idle games that lean toward growing the shop. In airplane mode, Pizza Ready and Burger Please! both opened an offline reward window first.

Where do your two games fit?

If you like hearing an order and baking it by hand in seven steps, pick Chichi's Baking. If you prefer growing the shop, pick Chichi's Idle Bakery Tycoon. Both were launched from builds with saved data.
