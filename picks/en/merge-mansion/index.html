Games Like Merge Mansion

Written and checked by Justin Lee · Checked 2026-10-30 · Device Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5 (SM-F731N)

We installed eight merge games on a Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5 and launched each one ourselves on October 30, 2026. Only three reopened in airplane mode (Merge Beach, Merge Dragons and our own bakery game), while Merge Mansion itself sat at 50% on connecting to the server. The table also notes whether an energy meter or a story scene showed on the first screen.

Three things tend to decide which merge game you pick next: whether an energy meter limits how much you can merge, whether a story drives you forward, and whether it opens with no signal. Merge Mansion, our yardstick, opens on a cutscene of the heroine entering the house, then in airplane mode its connecting bar stayed at 50% for the full 65 seconds.

One store label did not match what we saw. Merge Inn carries the Offline label on Google Play, yet in airplane mode it never got past the loading screen in our check. That is a 65-second watch, so a longer wait might differ. A lightning-bolt energy meter showed on the first screen of Merge Inn, Travel Town and Chichi's Cozy Merge Games, while a cutscene or dialogue came first in Merge Beach and Gossip Harbor.

Merge Beach swaps the mansion for a seaside house and has you follow island gossip as you clean it up. The store text says every merge reveals a clue, so a story pulls you along much as in Merge Mansion. The setting is a beach, and the player base is still small.

The first launch plays a sunset cutscene of the house, then a talk between the heroine and a taxi driver. In airplane mode, where Merge Mansion stalled, the same cutscene and dialogue opened. We only saw the intro.

  • Good The story starts on the first screen and still opens in airplane mode.
  • Weak spot It has 100K+ installs and about 3,800 ratings, so there are few outside reviews.
  • Best for People who want a Merge Mansion style story that also opens offline.

Travel Town has you help 55 villagers and bring a seaside town back to life. The store text promises over 500 objects across hundreds of levels. Where Merge Mansion follows one family's secrets, this one works through the townsfolk's requests one by one.

The first launch opens a tutorial board that asks you to merge two items, with a lightning-bolt energy number on screen. In airplane mode loading stopped at 26% with a connection-lost notice, so like Merge Mansion it needs internet to start.

  • Good The tutorial starts with merging two items right away, so the rules are easy to pick up.
  • Weak spot The energy meter shows from the first screen, and it would not open in airplane mode.
  • Best for People who like clearing villagers' requests and play on Wi-Fi.

Merge Inn puts you in a small diner with Maisie, merging food items to fill customer orders and grow the place. A diner is a long way from Merge Mansion's mystery, and the weight sits on expanding the menu.

The first screen is a cooking tutorial board with a lightning-bolt energy number. Google Play shows the Offline label, but our check did not match it. In airplane mode the loading bar crept forward and the board never opened within the 65 seconds we watched. We did not test a longer wait.

  • Good Merging food to fill orders is a change of scene for anyone tired of mansions.
  • Weak spot The energy meter shows on the first screen, and despite the Offline label it never opened a board in airplane mode.
  • Best for People who prefer fixing up a diner over a mansion and have a connection.

Chichi's Cozy Merge Games (치치의 빵 합치기) is our own merge game. Generators like a dough bowl or a loaf pan give out ingredients, and matching pairs climb from egg dough through butter rolls and red bean buns to a mammoth bun. Orders carry the customer's face, coins go into 30 repairs of a run-down bakery, and there is no long mystery like Merge Mansion's.

The first screen shows energy at 98 with a recharge timer. Relaunched in airplane mode it opened the same board and the timer kept counting down. That was a build already installed on our phone, not the same yardstick as the others.

  • Good The board opens in airplane mode and the energy timer keeps running.
  • Weak spot It carries ads and has no store rating yet, and with airplane mode off the phone jumped twice to what looked like other games' ads within 100 seconds.
  • Best for People who want a light merge about filling bread orders, not a long story.

Gossip Harbor follows Quinn Castillo as her picture-perfect island life falls apart, with you restoring and decorating her restaurant. You merge coffee, sandwiches and seafood to serve customers, and the store text leads with a mystery of divorce and sabotage. That story weight is close to Merge Mansion.

The first launch ran a dialogue scene between the characters. Soon after, the phone jumped once to another game's Play Store page (our blind tap may have hit an ad). In airplane mode a network error said the connection failed, and it went no further.

  • Good Dialogue opens on the first screen, and 100M+ installs with about 730,000 ratings means plenty of reviews.
  • Weak spot It stopped at a network error in airplane mode.
  • Best for People who want a mystery to follow while decorating a restaurant, with a connection.

Merge County sends you back to your childhood home in Newburyton to rebuild the town by merging materials. The store text says each villager has their own tasks and new areas open as you level up. It spreads across a whole town instead of one mansion as Merge Mansion does.

The first launch opens a tutorial on the island map that points at the first task. In airplane mode a network error window said it could not reach the server and offered only exit or retry.

  • Good New areas open as you level up, per the store text, so the next goal is always clear.
  • Weak spot A network error window blocked it in airplane mode, leaving only exit or retry.

How we checked

We installed all eight on our work phone and took the first-launch and airplane-mode screens ourselves. Airplane mode means relaunching with it switched on and watching for 65 seconds. Energy and story notes cover only what showed on the first screen, and we did not count ads.

Chichi's Cozy Merge Games (치치의 빵 합치기) ran from a build already installed on the phone, which is not a fresh download and not the same yardstick as the rest. We did not rank our own game first, and its weak points get the same space as the others.

FAQ

Which games like Merge Mansion work without internet?

Three of eight reopened in airplane mode: Merge Beach, Merge Dragons and Chichi's Cozy Merge Games. Merge Beach played its intro cutscene and dialogue, and Merge Dragons opened its tutorial island. The other five stopped at a loading bar or a network error.

Which of these show an energy meter?

A lightning-bolt energy number was visible on the first screen of Merge Inn, Travel Town and Chichi's Cozy Merge Games. We did not check the others for energy, so look at their store pages before you install.

Which ones open on a story like Merge Mansion does?

Merge Beach and Gossip Harbor showed a cutscene or character dialogue on the first screen. Merge Beach has a sunset seaside-house scene and a taxi driver talk, while Gossip Harbor opened on a conversation between characters. Gossip Harbor did not open in airplane mode.

Who is Chichi's Cozy Merge Games for?

People who want a light merge game about combining bread ingredients to fill customer orders, not a long mystery. It uses an energy meter, carries ads and has no store rating yet. All of that comes from the build installed on our work phone.
