Games Like Hay Day

Written and checked by Justin Lee · Checked 2026-10-30 · Device Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5 (SM-F731N)

We installed all eight games on a Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5 and launched each one ourselves on October 30, 2026. Four of the eight reopened in airplane mode (Township, FarmVille 2 and our two bakery games), while Hay Day stopped at a connection failure notice. If you want a farm you can open without internet, start with the airplane column in the table.

Hay Day is about planting crops, raising animals and filling orders with what you make. It also asks on first launch whether you have played before, and in airplane mode it only shows a notice that it cannot reach the server. So it helps to judge two things apart: how close a game feels to Hay Day, and whether it opens with no signal.

This list has six farm games and two bakery games from our own studio. Among the farms, Township and FarmVille 2 opened offline, while Family Farm Seaside, Family Island, Big Farm and Spring Valley stalled. That fits the usual pattern that server-based farm games tend to get stuck without a connection.

Township is less a pure farm than a town builder that starts on the farm. You grow crops, feed factories and spend the output on houses and community buildings, and the store text also mentions match-3 levels. Compared with Hay Day's tidy farm it gives you more systems to juggle.

The first screen is a prompt to plow a field. Relaunched in airplane mode it opened the same tutorial, which set it apart from the other server-style farm games that got stuck.

  • Good The first screen opens in airplane mode, so it is easy to try where signal is poor.
  • Weak spot We only reached the tutorial, so how far a save plays offline is unchecked.
  • Best for People who want a bigger game that adds town building to farming.

FarmVille 2 is the closest to Hay Day's order loop: crops and trees feed workshops, and the goods are sold on order boards. Running several workshops for milk, eggs and cheese feels familiar too. What we saw, though, was only the intro that has you repair the farmhouse.

The first launch asks for notification permission. In airplane mode it still opened to that repair tutorial, so unlike Hay Day it did not end on a connection failure.

  • Good The Hay Day style loop of filling orders with crafted goods can at least be started offline.
  • Weak spot The installed app declares 18 permissions, the most on this list, which is worth checking before you install.
  • Best for People who want Hay Day's order delivery loop in another farm.

Family Farm Seaside has you build a farm by the shore and raise animals and crops, and its store text pushes 200 products and unlimited barn space. The grow-and-harvest core is close to Hay Day, with a seaside setting that changes the look.

The first launch opens a tutorial farm about the first harvest. In airplane mode it showed an internet connection error and never reached the game, which is where it matches Hay Day.

  • Good The tutorial reaches a first harvest quickly, so you get the farming loop early.
  • Weak spot It stops at a connection error in airplane mode, so it needs internet to open.
  • Best for People who will play on Wi-Fi and want a harvest game close to Hay Day.

Family Island is not only a farm. You guide a Stone Age family on a desert island, building and farming as you open up land, and the store text also mentions puzzles. It leans on adventure more than Hay Day's quiet farm tending.

The first launch opens the island tutorial, with a speech bubble for the first task. In airplane mode it stayed on the title screen for the full 65 seconds.

  • Good A speech bubble points to the first task, so a new player does not get lost.
  • Weak spot It never left the title screen in airplane mode, and the install is a large 403 MB.
  • Best for People who want farming mixed with an exploration story.

Chichi's Idle Bakery Tycoon (치치의 빵집 타이쿤) is our own idle bakery game. You start with one oven and one shelf and grow a neighborhood shop around salt bread. You tend a shop instead of fields, so only the sell-and-expand feel overlaps with Hay Day.

Relaunched in airplane mode it resumed the saved bakery on day 13 and rolled over to day 14 about a minute later. That was a build already installed on our phone with a saved game, not a fresh install.

  • Good A saved game reopens in airplane mode and the days keep moving.
  • Weak spot It carries ads and has no store rating yet, so there are no outside reviews to lean on.
  • Best for People who prefer slowly growing a shop over a farm.

  • Good The team play suits anyone who liked the cooperative side of Hay Day.

Spring Valley is an adventure-leaning farm game: you build a farm in a valley and follow quests across its landscapes. The store text puts crops, animals and quests up front, but the weight is on moving the story, which is where it differs from Hay Day.

The first launch opens a privacy consent window. In airplane mode it shows a no-connection notice asking you to check the internet and restart.

How we checked

We installed every game on our work phone and took the first-launch and airplane-mode screens ourselves. Airplane mode means relaunching the app with it switched on and watching for 65 seconds. We did not count ads, so the ad column only copies what each store page labels.

Our two games ran from a build already installed on our phone with a saved game, which is not a fresh download and not the same yardstick as the rest. For Chichi's Baking our taps missed the open-shop button, so we only confirmed the first screen.

FAQ

Which Hay Day alternatives work without internet?

Four of eight reopened in airplane mode: Township, FarmVille 2, Chichi's Idle Bakery Tycoon and Chichi's Baking. Township and FarmVille 2 got as far as the tutorial. Family Farm Seaside, Family Island, Big Farm and Spring Valley stopped at a connection notice or a loading bar.

Does Hay Day itself work in airplane mode?

Not on our Galaxy Z Flip5 on October 30, 2026. Relaunched in airplane mode it showed a connection failure saying it could not reach the server and went no further.

Which of these show no ads on the store page?

Township and Family Farm Seaside list in-app purchases without an ads label. The other six list ads. We did not count ads in the first ten minutes, though an ad screen did open once within 100 seconds in Big Farm.

Where do your two games fit?

Chichi's Idle Bakery Tycoon suits people happy to run a shop instead of a farm, and its saved game resumed in airplane mode. Chichi's Baking is a hands-on bakery where you hear an order and bake it, so it is further from farm management.
