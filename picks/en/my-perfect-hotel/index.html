Games Like My Perfect Hotel

Written and checked by Justin Lee · Checked 2026-11-13 · Device Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5 (SM-F731N)

We launched nine hotel and shop games to play after My Perfect Hotel (SayGames) on a Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5 on November 13, 2026. Six of the nine opened a game screen when relaunched in airplane mode. Hotel Empire Tycoon, Merge Hotel Empire and Idle Apartment Tycoon stopped on server connection errors. My Perfect Hotel itself reopened to its front desk in airplane mode.

My Perfect Hotel has you running around as the owner, cleaning rooms and restocking toilet paper until there is enough cash to hire staff and step back. People who want more of it usually want one of two things: the hands-on arcade running, or a calmer idle game where the numbers climb without you.

So we split the picks by who does the work. Hotel Manager, Burger Please and Monkey Mart keep you running yourself. Grand Hotel Mania is time management, meeting guest requests on a clock. Hotel Empire Tycoon, Idle Hotel Kingdom and Idle Apartment Tycoon are floor-by-floor idlers, and Merge Hotel Empire decorates a hotel through merge puzzles. Our Korean Spa Tycoon is in because it is also a place that serves guests.

This is the closest match to the benchmark. It comes from Supercent, and you walk an owner down the hallways, prepare rooms and take guests in the same top-down view. The difference is the opening: a short exchange between two characters, Elise and David, and then a choice between a male and a female owner. Think of it as My Perfect Hotel with a little story attached.

On our phone the dialogue led to the character picker, then to a blue-carpeted hallway with a 'prepare a room 0/1' task. Relaunched in airplane mode, the same hallway and task opened. The install is 235 MB, close to the benchmark's 257 MB, and one permission prompt appeared after the first launch.

  • Good The hands-on running feels almost the same, so switching is easy.
  • Weak spot We only reached the first task, so we did not see how it plays after hiring staff.
  • Best for Players who finished My Perfect Hotel and want another hotel just like it.

Burger Please is another Supercent game, with the hotel swapped for a burger shop. You carry ingredients and work the counter yourself until there is enough cash to bring in staff, the same order as the benchmark. Only the subject changes, so the controls you already know carry over. It suits players who liked the running-the-place part more than the hotel theme itself.

When we launched it on October 30, the first screen prompted us to collect cash and opened the first path. Relaunched in airplane mode, an offline reward window came up first, and the double-reward button asked for gems or a video view. Closing it left the same start screen with the cash at 28. The install is 209 MB.

  • Good It launches without internet and still hands out an offline reward.
  • Weak spot Doubling that reward needs a video or gems.
  • Best for Players who want a hands-on shop game and do not mind it not being a hotel.

This one is ours. You run a Korean bathhouse, adding baths and sauna rooms one at a time and growing the place with what guests spend while they rest. It is not a running game. You tap facilities to upgrade them in an idle loop, so it overlaps with My Perfect Hotel on serving guests and growing a business, not on the hands-on feel. That shared guest-business angle is why it is here.

We launched the build already on our work phone, with saved progress. Relaunched in airplane mode, the bathhouse opened and income rose from 6,478 to 6,999 won over 65 seconds, with the first tutorial bubble still showing. A fresh install may look different, so this was not measured the same way as the others.

  • Good Guests keep arriving and income keeps rising with no connection.
  • Weak spot You never move a character yourself.
  • Best for Players who want a slower, idle shop instead of a hotel rush.

A monkey clerk runs a small market here. You drag to move, buy shelves one at a time, stock them with goods like bananas and stand at the checkout, which is the same loop as cleaning rooms in My Perfect Hotel. The art is simple and cute, so it also works for playing with kids. It is the mobile version of a game first known in browsers.

The first launch puts the monkey on a sandy lot in front of a 'MONKEY MART' gate, with a first shelf spot priced at 100 and a drag-to-move hint. You start with 350 cash. Relaunched in airplane mode, the same lot opened. At 70 MB it was the smallest install of the ten we checked, and there is a separate remove-ads button on the left.

  • Good Light at 70 MB and picks up right away without internet.
  • Weak spot It is a market, not a hotel, so there are no rooms to decorate.
  • Best for Players short on phone storage or looking for something light to share with kids.

This is time management set in a hotel. Each level runs on a clock, and you score by tapping arriving guests to greet them, taking them to rooms and meeting their requests in time. Instead of growing one hotel for a long stretch like My Perfect Hotel, you clear short levels one by one, so it fits players who like busy hands. It plays in landscape.

The first launch opens an 'Are you 18 or older?' window on a black screen. Our tool tapped no and hit an age-restriction window, so we tapped yes by hand. Past a terms window and a notification prompt, play opened a lobby level on a 60-second clock, and the airplane-mode relaunch opened the same level. At 403 MB it was the largest install.

  • Good Levels are timed, which makes short sessions easy.
  • Weak spot Answer no on the age window and the game will not let you play at all.
  • Best for Players who prefer busy time management over relaxed idling.

Codigames made this idle hotel. You never steer a character. Instead you tap rooms one by one and upgrade furniture such as the bed, nightstand and TV to raise hourly income. The theme matches the benchmark but the feel is the opposite, so it suits players who found the running around tiring. The screens look a lot like the studio's Idle Supermarket Tycoon.

  • Good Every piece of furniture shows its income, so progress is easy to read.

How we checked

Seven of the nine were freshly installed on our work phone on November 13, and we captured the first launch and an airplane-mode relaunch ourselves. The benchmark was checked the same way. Burger Please carries over a record taken the same way on October 30. For the airplane-mode check we closed the app, switched airplane mode on, reopened it and watched for 65 seconds.

We did not count ads. The ads column only copies each store page label. On Grand Hotel Mania our tool tapped no on the age window and got blocked, so we relaunched and tapped yes by hand. Korean Spa Tycoon is our own game, run from the build already on the phone with saved progress, so it is not measured by the same yardstick as the rest.

FAQ

Does My Perfect Hotel work without internet?

On a Galaxy Z Flip5 on November 13, 2026, relaunching it in airplane mode reopened the front desk screen with the 'take a guest at the front desk' task. We did not play past that first task. Google Play also shows an offline label for it.

Which of these did not open without internet?

Hotel Empire Tycoon stopped on a connection error window (code 4), Merge Hotel Empire on a server connection error at about 10 percent loading, and Idle Apartment Tycoon on an 'Error_SDK_Init_Failed' warning. The other six opened a game screen in airplane mode.

Which ones keep the hands-on running of My Perfect Hotel?

Hotel Manager, Burger Please and Monkey Mart have you move a character and run the place yourself. Hotel Manager is the closest, with the same hotel theme and camera angle. Hotel Empire Tycoon, Idle Hotel Kingdom and Idle Apartment Tycoon are tap-to-upgrade idle games.

Which game is the smallest download?

By the APK total we measured, Monkey Mart was the smallest at 70 MB and Grand Hotel Mania the largest at 403 MB. My Perfect Hotel was 257 MB. Extra data downloaded after the first launch can differ from game to game.
