Games Like Idle Miner Tycoon

Written and checked by Justin Lee · Checked 2026-10-30 · Device Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5 (SM-F731N)

We launched all ten idle games on a Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5 on October 30, 2026. Five reopened to a game screen in airplane mode (Earth Inc., Egg, Inc., Office Cat and our two games), and Idle Theme Park Tycoon stopped on a network error. The other four (AdVenture Capitalist, Dev Tycoon, Idle Bank Tycoon, Idle Restaurant Tycoon) halted at a terms, age or nickname window, so we saw nothing past it.

Idle Miner Tycoon is about sinking deeper into a mine, adding workers, and watching cash pile up while you are away. People looking for something similar usually want two answers: does it feel the same, and will it open where there is no signal, like a subway or a flight. This page leans on the second answer, checked on a real phone.

The ten split into four mine-like idlers, four games about running a shop or company, and two of ours. Two of them, Idle Bank Tycoon and Idle Restaurant Tycoon, come from Kolibri Games, the studio behind Idle Miner Tycoon, and both open with a similar age-check window. Same publisher, similar start.

Earth Inc. is the closest to Idle Miner Tycoon's look: you dig rocks for cash, hire workers, and the store text promises a mining firm that grows into a galactic conglomerate. Cash builds up while you are away, so switching over is easy. It opens with a boardroom cutscene instead.

After the cutscene a prompt tells you to tap a rock to mine. Relaunched in airplane mode it opened the same tutorial screen. We only saw the intro.

  • Good The familiar mine-idle loop can at least be started with no signal.
  • Weak spot We stopped at the tutorial, so how far the game plays offline is unknown.
  • Best for People who want to keep digging in another mine idler.

Egg, Inc. swaps the mine for an egg farm. You hatch chickens, build hen houses and hire drivers to ship eggs, and the store text sells crisp 3D graphics and a swarm of hens in place of menus. Where Idle Miner Tycoon digs down, this one reaches out to space.

Relaunched in airplane mode it played the space intro and reached the first tutorial screen with the shipping warehouse. Some Korean text on screen turned into boxes, and we only got through the first minutes.

  • Good With over 1.4 million ratings it is well covered, so guides and reviews are easy to find.
  • Weak spot Some Korean text showed as boxes offline, which made the tutorial harder to read.
  • Best for People who want an idle game with a theme other than mining.

AdVenture Capitalist starts at a lemonade stand and works up to a multinational. You buy business after business and hand them to managers, rather than digging mine levels, so only the hand-it-to-a-manager feel matches Idle Miner Tycoon. The store text also says outfits for your capitalist boost profit.

The first launch opens a privacy and terms window, and the agree-and-play button only unlocks after you scroll to the end. The same window opened in airplane mode. We did not scroll through it, so the game after it is unseen.

  • Good The lemonade-stand start is simple enough to need no explanation.
  • Weak spot A long terms window must be scrolled before play, and our check ended there.
  • Best for People curious about buying up businesses instead of digging levels.

Idle Theme Park Tycoon grows a small amusement park into a famous theme park. The store text says managers and automation keep cash coming while you are away, and you return to collect and reinvest. It upgrades rides one by one instead of mine levels, but the collect-and-reinvest rhythm matches Idle Miner Tycoon.

Office Cat is a cute idle game where cat employees run the office. You start in a small office, swap furniture and decorate rooms as it grows into a global company, putting more weight on decorating than Idle Miner Tycoon's mine does. The save-and-reinvest backbone is the same.

PC Cafe Tycoon (PC방 키우기) is our own idle game. You inherit a back-alley internet cafe, start with six seats, and place the ramen counter and vending machines yourself. The store text says they sell more the closer they sit to the seats, so layout becomes income. Staff keeping the shop running overnight is what it shares with Idle Miner Tycoon; placing things by hand is what it adds.

Relaunched in airplane mode, the shop screen still opened and the cash counter rose from 14,490 to 25,098 won in about 65 seconds. This is the development build already on our work phone, with saved data.

How we checked

We installed all ten on our work phone and took the first-launch and airplane-mode screens ourselves. Airplane mode means relaunching the app with it switched on and watching for 65 seconds. We did not tap through terms or age windows for the games, so a game that stopped there is marked blocked, which does not mean it fails. We did not count ads. The table only copies the ads label from each store page.

Our two games (PC Cafe Tycoon and Korean Spa Tycoon) ran from builds already installed on our phone with saved games. A brand-new install may look different, and they are not measured by the same yardstick as the rest.

FAQ

Which Idle Miner Tycoon alternatives work without internet?

Five of ten reopened to a game screen in airplane mode: Earth Inc., Egg, Inc., Office Cat, PC Cafe Tycoon and Korean Spa Tycoon. Earth Inc. and Egg, Inc. reached their tutorials, and Office Cat's cash kept climbing for the full 65 seconds.

Which game clearly failed in airplane mode?

Idle Theme Park Tycoon. Online it reached the ride upgrade screen, but relaunched in airplane mode it stopped on the loading screen with a network error, code 4. The four blocked games halted at consent or setup windows, so we make no call on them.

Do any of these show no ads label on the store page?

All ten list ads on the store page. We did not count how many ads appear in the first ten minutes. No ad window showed up in Office Cat during its 65 seconds in airplane mode.

Where do your two games fit?

If running a shop sounds better than running a mine, PC Cafe Tycoon and Korean Spa Tycoon fit. Both resumed a saved game in airplane mode, though that was a build already on our phone, and neither has a store rating yet.
