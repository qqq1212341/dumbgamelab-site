Dumb Game Lab

Dumb Game Lab is an independent mobile game studio based in the Seoul Capital Area, South Korea. The studio turns everyday places — the neighborhood PC cafe, the local bathhouse, the corner bakery — into cozy idle tycoons and casual games, and ships every title in eight languages on the App Store and Google Play.

About

The studio

Dumb Game Lab started with one question: what is actually fun about running the places we grew up in? Each game picks one everyday Korean place, finds the single decision that makes running it fun, and polishes that until it feels good in your hand. Concept, art, code, sound and localization are all made in-house.

Featured game

Idle Internet Cafe Tycoon

App StoreGoogle PlayPlay on the web

Take over your uncle's struggling back-alley PC cafe. Place gaming seats, ramen counters and vending machines, hire part-timers, and grow a six-seat alley shop into the best PC cafe in the country.

  • One decision, played well: buy equipment or hire staff
  • Placement is profit — ramen counters sell more the closer they sit to the seats; block the aisle and customers can't get through
  • Six stages of growth, from back alley to nationwide brand, each with its own story
  • Staff keep the shop running while the app is closed
  • Two-minute days, up to 3x speed, playable offline
